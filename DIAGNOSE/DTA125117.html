Diagnosis Steps

1
Check the electronic smart key.
  1. Check whether the battery of the electronic smart key is installed reversely and whether the power is normal.
  2. Check whether the electronic smart key battery holder is kept in improper contact or damaged.
  3. Check whether the waterproof test paper on the PCB of electronic smart key is discolored.
  4. Check the PCB of the electronic smart key for oxidation or corrosion.
    5. Repace电子智能钥匙 。
2
Check the wiring harness and connector of the front right door handle microswitch.
  1. Set the START/STOP button to OFF.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector of right front door handle micro switch U10.
  3. Check whether the wiring harness and connector are normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
3
Check the right front door handle micro switch.
  1. Press and hold the front right door handle microswitch.
  2. Measure the resistance between the right front door handle micro switch U10-5 and U10-6.
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
