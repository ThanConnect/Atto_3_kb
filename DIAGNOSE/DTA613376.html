B17941B Front right far side airbag module not connected

DTC Description

B17941B Front right far side airbag module not connected

Fault symptoms

The front right far-side airbag module function fails.

Possible fault causes

  1. Wiring harness or connector fault

  2. The front right far-side airbag module faults.

  3. Airbag control module fault.

Fault setting conditions

The resistance of front right far-side airbag module is 0.

Fault triggering conditions

The airbag controller receives the signal that the front right far-side airbag module resistance is 0, and a DTC is generated.

Diagnosis Steps

1
Check the DTC of SRS.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Clear the DTC.
  4. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC.
  6. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    7. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the wiring harness connector of side airbag (driver side).
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector K96(A) from the side airbag (driver side).
  3. Check whether the wiring harness connector of side airbag (driver side) is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
3
Check the wiring harness and connector of airbag controller.
  1. Disconnect the wiring harness connector KG10_1 from the airbag controller.
  2. Check whether the wiring harness and connector of airbag control module are normal.
    3. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
4
Check whether the driver inner side airbag circuit is short-circuited to GND.

  1. Measure the resistance between the driver inner side airbag harness connector K96(A)-1 and the ground.
  2. Measure the resistance between the driver inner side airbag harness connector K96(A)-2 and the ground.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    K96(A)-1 GND Always Above 10 kΩ
    K96(A)-2
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
5
Check whether the driver inner side airbag circuit is short-circuited to power supply.

  1. Set the START/STOP button to ON
  2. Measure the voltage between the driver inner side airbag harness connector K96(A)-1 and the ground.
  3. Measure the voltage between the driver inner side airbag harness connector K96(A)-2 and the ground.
    Connector Condition Voltage
    (+) (-)
    K96(A)-1 GND Always Not exist
    K96(A)-2
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
6
Check whether the circuit of the driver inner side airbag is open.

  1. Measure the resistance between SRS controller harness connector KG10_1–8 and front passenger inner side airbag harness connector K96(A)-2.
  2. Measure the resistance between SRS controller harness connector KG10_1–39 and front passenger inner side airbag harness connector K96(A)-1.
    Connector Condition Resistance
    (+) (-)
    KG10_1–8 K96(A)-2 Always Below 1 Ω
    KG10_1–39 K96(A)-1
  3. Check whether the result is normal.
    4. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
7
Replace the driver inner side airbag and check the DTC.
  1. Replace the driver inner side airbag and restore the vehicle.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Read the DTC of SRS.
  4. Clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again and read the DTC of SRS.
  7. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
