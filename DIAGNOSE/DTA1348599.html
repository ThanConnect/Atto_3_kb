P2B466C DC abnormal, unable to power on to OK mode

DTC Description

P2B466C DC abnormal, unable to power on to OK mode

Fault symptoms

-

Possible fault causes

Integrated front drive controller fault

Fault setting conditions

Communication fault with DC

Fault triggering conditions

When communication failure with DC is detected， the DTC will be generated.

Diagnosis Steps

1
Check the DTC of power domain control unit.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the power domain control unit interface, and read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again.
  7. Enter the power domain control unit interface again, and read the DTC.
  8. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    9. Check for "intermittent and occasional fault".
2
Check the wiring harness connector of the CCS communication converter
  1. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position.
  2. Disconnect the wiring harness connector of CCS communication converter B77.
  3. Check for corrosion, damage, or pin withdrawal.
  4. Check whether the result is normal.
    5. Repair or replace the wiring harness.
3
Check whether the wiring harness connector of the CCS communication converter is short-circuited to GND.
  1. Measure the resistance between CCS communication converter harness connector and ground.

6
Check the DTC of DC-DC assembly.
  1. Connect the VDS to the DLC.
  2. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position.
  3. Scan the ECU module.
  4. Enter the DC-DC assembly interface, and read and clear the DTC.
  5. Turn the Start/Stop switch to OFF position and wait for a few seconds.
  6. Turn the Start/Stop switch to ON position again.
  7. Go to DC-DC assembly again and read the DTC.
  8. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    9. Check for other DC-DC DTCs.
7
Check the integrated front drive controller and CCS communication converter software.
  1. Enter the integrated front drive controller and CCS communication converter and use VDS to update software.
  2. Enter the integrated front drive controller and CCS communication converter again to read the DTC.
  3. Check whether the same DTC is displayed.
    4. Replace the integrated front drive controller and battery execution and sampling unit.
