Adjust the spot welding equipment.

You can apply the working mode of the welding equipment to the corresponding scenario by learning the following key information.

  • Welding current: Select the corresponding welding current according to the welding conditions (plate thickness, welding position, welding speed, material, and other parameters).

  • Arc voltage:

    • In order to obtain a good welding effect, it is necessary to have an appropriate arc length, which depends on the arc voltage.

    • If the arc voltage is too low, the welding wire will not generate an arc, but will stick to the steel panel, producing a bang-bang sound. This results in deeper fusion depth, increased welding slag, and higher weld bead.

    • If the arc voltage is appropriate, not only a good welding effect can be obtained, but also a smooth squeaking sound can be heard.

    • If the arc voltage is too high, the arc length will increase. In addition to increasing the amount of welding slag and noise, it will also lead to welding defects such as wider weld pool width and shallower fusion depth.

  • Wire feeding speed:

    • Wire feeding speed determines the filling amount of the weld pool. It shall be properly adjusted according to the current value.

    • Slow feeding will result in discontinuous arc; and fast feeding will lead to a bang-bang sound as the welding gun will kick back.

  • Welding power-on time:

    Caution

    Generally, adjust this switch to "0" during sheet metal repair and welding. And the operator controls the power-on time of welding.

    • The carbon dioxide gas shielded welder has the function of welding time control.

    • Turn on this switch, and the welding time is intelligently controlled by the welder itself.

    • Turn off this switch and adjust the time knob to "0". The welding power-on time is controlled by the operator.

  • Gas flow:

    Caution

    Carbon dioxide welding is not recommended in a place with high wind speed, otherwise, it may cause poor protection of welding spots by the shielding gas.

    • The standard flow rate currently used is in the range of 10-15 L/min.

    • The flow rate shall also be adjusted based on the distance from the nozzle to the base metal, the welding current, the welding speed, and the surrounding environment (wind speed).

  • Distance between electrode and base metal:

    Caution

    • The general standard distance between the welding gun and the panel is about 8-15 mm.

    • Never use the nozzle as a fulcrum to contact the panel during welding.

    • The distance between the electrode and base metal is another important factor affecting the welding effect.

    • If the distance is too large, the welding wire will melt faster. This is because the protruding length of the welding wire is too long, and the excessively long part will generate preheating. Therefore, the current flow rate will decrease, thus reducing the penetration depth of weld beads. At the same time, excessive distance will also reduce the isolation effect of shielding gas.

    • If the distance is too small, it will be difficult for the operator to observe the welding area, thus affecting the welding quality.

Welding skills

  • Gun holding posture:

    Caution

    The grasping position should not be too close to the nozzle, so as not to affect the vision and avoid burns.

    • Use the thumb and index finger mainly in the manipulation of the welding gun.

    • Hold the gun with one hand and hold the handle of the welding gun with another hand to control the welding spots.

  • Forward welding: The angle of push welding is about 10°-15° to the vertical plane of the base metal. It is characterized by shallow fusion depth and high weld beads.

  • Backward welding: The angle of push welding is about 30° to the vertical plane of the base metal. It is characterized by deep fusion depth and flat weld beads.

  • Welding speed:

    • During welding, the correct welding current and speed must be adjusted according to the thickness of the base metal to obtain a good fusion depth and weld bead width.

    • If the welding current remains unchanged, accelerating the welding speed will reduce the fusion depth and weld bead width, resulting in bead protruding and insufficient welding strength. If the welding speed is too slow, the base metal will be overheated, resulting in burn-through.

    • Generally, the welding speed of a thin steel panel with a thickness of 0.8 mm is 105-115 mm/min. Usually, the thicker the steel panel, the slower the welding speed.

  • Butt welding:

    Operation instructions:

    • Butt welding is relatively simple, but prolonged high temperatures may easily cause deformation at the welding position.

    • During welding, the method of reserved weld and fixed-position welding can be adopted, and preferably, the welding shall be performed section by section, and special attention shall be paid to the welding sequence.

    • Welding shall be carried out in a cross or skip welding sequence as much as possible.

    • For butt welding, ensure the flatness of panels.

    Welding requirements:

    • Make tack welding as required and ensure that the panels are not deformed.

    • Continuous seam welding shall be carried out in sections to minimize deformation.

    • Although butt welding can be carried out for both thick and thin steel panels, in order to ensure better permeability when welding thicker steel panels, the opening must be ground into a bevel.

    Welding methods:

    Caution

    • This method is applied to the parts where overlap welding cannot be carried out.

    • This welding method must be used on the exterior structural parts of the vehicle body to ensure the flatness of the exterior parts.

    1. Place two steel panels on the same plane, and fill the gap between them for connection.

    2. It is used on welding panels to be joined after cutting.

  • Lap welding:

    Operation instructions:

    • Lap welding is relatively simple, and it is necessary to ensure that the gap between panels is kept tight before welding.

    • Resistance spot welding or gas shielded welding can be used for lap welding.

    • Where gas shielded welding is adopted, temporary welding can be performed for fixing so as to prevent deformation. If plug welding is used, holes shall be drilled in advance and the edges of holes shall be deburred.

    • It is mostly used for parts of the vehicle body where spot welding or plug welding cannot be carried out.

    • This method is usually used for welding structural parts inside the vehicle body.

    Welding requirements:

    • Make sure that there is no poor soldering at the welding spots.

    • No obvious porosity and burn-through.

    Welding method: Lap welding is a welding method in which the edges of two overlapping steel panels are welded to combine them into a whole.

  • Lining plate welding:

    Caution

    When welding the lining plates, avoid gaps between the upper and lower panels, otherwise, the interface position may be deformed.

    Operation instructions:

    • The use of lining plates can avoid thermal deformation and improve welding strength. Resistance spot welding or plug welding can be used for welding the overlapping parts of lining plates.

    • Lining plate welding is often used to weld local areas with long lengths and small shape differences and where greater welding strength is required.

    Welding requirements:

    • The welds of lining plate welding shall have the same surface profile and be free from defects such as pores.

    • The heat-affected range (discoloration area) is between 20-30 times the plate thickness.

    • There is no excessive penetration depth on the back, but obvious weld marks are required.

  • Plug welding:

    Caution

    The welding gun shall be perpendicular to the panel.

    Operation instructions:

    • Plug welding is the most commonly used welding method in vehicle body repair. It is applied to special positions where spot welding cannot be carried out or positions where the strength requirements cannot be met by spot welding.

    • Drill holes in the upper plate of two or more overlapping steel panels, and then fill them.

    Welding requirements:

    • Weld nugget diameter ≤ hole diameter + 2 mm

    • There is penetration on the back.

    • It shall be free of defects such as burnout, incomplete filling, and porosity.

    • Plug hole height < 2 mm

    Plug welding method for small holes (about 6 mm in diameter):

    • Align the welding wire with the center of the hole and activate the switch of the welding gun.

    • Fill the hole and release the welding gun switch.

    • The diameter of the plug welding used on the vehicle body is 6 mm for the outer panel and 8 mm for the inner structural plate.

Welding process

  1. Positioning panel: After positioning the steel panel to be replaced, fix it with a locking clamp.

    Caution

    • To prevent panel deformation, the locking clamp shall not be too tight during positioning.

    • When selecting a locking clamp, make sure that the clamping surface is not too narrow and there shall be no foreign matters on the surface.

    • Use U-shaped and wide-plane locking pliers to clamp panels and ensure that there is no gap at the joint position.

  2. Welder commissioning

    • Adjust various functions of the welder according to the user's manual.

    • Test welding shall be carried out with test plates of the same material and thickness.

    • Place the nozzle end close to the steel panel.

    • Turn the welding gun switch to "ON", and contact the end of the welding wire with the steel panel to generate an arc.

    • First, confirm the welder is adjusted correctly, and then carry out panel welding.

  3. Tack welding: Temporary spot welding can pre-position two steel panels.

    Caution

    The temporary welding spacing is generally 15-30 times the thickness of the welded plate.

  4. Welding in the sequence from 1 to 5 is helpful for heat dissipation of steel panels to avoid deformation.

  5. Main welding.

    • Move in a stable posture to prevent the welding gun from shaking.

    • Align with the end of tack welding.

    • Repeatedly turn the switch to "ON" and "OFF".

    • Conduct continuous seam welding on the welding spot subject to tack welding.

  6. Connecting welding points: Welding by aligning the welding wire with the end of the previous welding spot not only produces a good welding appearance, but also effectively controls welding heat and prevents deformation.

    Caution

    Weld the latter welding spot at about two-thirds of the previous welding spot.

  7. Perform anti-corrosion treatment. Refer to Anti-corrosion treatment 

    Caution

    Anti-corrosion treatment is a necessary process. Ignoring the anti-corrosion treatment will not guarantee the repair quality.

FAQs and their solutions

  • Burn-through and excessive penetration depth:

    Cause:

    • Excessive current and voltage

    • The welding speed is too slow and the heat is excessively concentrated.

    • Unreasonable welding methods

    Solutions:

    • Adjust to reasonable current and voltage.

    • Increase the welding speed.

    • Select the appropriate forward welding or backward welding methods.

    • Welding lining plates can be used to prevent burn-through or excessive deformation of panels.

  • Insufficient melting point:

    Cause:

    • The current and voltage are too small.

    • The welding speed is too fast and the heat is insufficient.

    • Unreasonable welding methods

    Solutions:

    • Increase the gas flow (10-15 L/min).

    • Before welding, clean the welding area of the panel to remove oil and impurities.

    • Avoid welding in windy places.

  • Weld pass porosity:

    Cause:

    • Insufficient shielding gas flow

    • The welding area is not cleaned and has oil stains or other impurities.

    • There is wind during welding.

    Solutions:

    • Increase the gas flow (10-15 L/min).

    • Before welding, clean the welding area of the panel to remove oil and impurities.

    • Avoid welding in windy places.

  • Deviation of weld pass:

    Cause:

    • Unstable gun movement during welding

    • The angle of the welding gun is incorrect, blocking the sight.

    • No special welding mask is used.

    Solutions:

    • Operators should strengthen welding training.

    • Use the special welding mask to ensure the visibility of welding.

    • Adjust the welding angle and direction.

  • End undercut

    Cause:

    • It fails to produce an arc at the start position of welding.

    • It fails to produce an arc at the end position of welding.

    Solutions:

    • Use welding guide plates at the start and end positions.

    • The length of the guide plate shall not be less than 20 mm.

    • Welding starts and ends at the position of the guide plate.
