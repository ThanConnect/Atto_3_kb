Preliminary preparation

To ensure the operation quality, preliminary preparations shall be made before operation.

Caution

  • During operation, please take safety protection measures as required. For details of safety protection, please refer to Spraying protection 

  • Before operation, please carefully read and observe site safety and vehicle protection. For details, please refer to Site safety and vehicle protection 

Cleaning of spray booth

  • Clean the spray booth walls with compressed air to ensure that all dirt and dust have been removed.

  • Regularly check the oil-water separator of the air compressor, and drain water in time if necessary. Refer to Auxiliary equipment and tools 

  • Do not use silicon-containing products in the painting workshop or spray booth.

  • Regularly maintain the spray booth and replace the filter materials in it.

Cleaning yourself

Warning

Do not blow on your face or ears.

  • Reasonably wear workwear, caps and protective masks for spraying, remove foreign matters and dust adhered to your body from top to bottom by using compressed air with a pressure higher than the spraying pressure.

Paint mixing

Warning

  • Water-based paint shall be mixed in plastic containers or metal containers with coatings inside.

  • Improper thinner or incorrect thinning will result in paint sagging, coarse textures and other defects.

  • Uniformly mix curing agent, thinner and coloring paint suitable for the ambient temperature of the spray booth with an electronic scale (by weight) or a paint mixing ruler (by volume).

Subsequent operations

  • Spray solvent-based plain paint. Refer to Solvent-based plain paint 

  • Spray water-based plain paint. Refer to Water-based plain paint 

  • Spray solvent-based metallic paint. Refer to Solvent-based metallic paint 

  • Spray water-based metallic paint. Refer to Water-based metallic paint
