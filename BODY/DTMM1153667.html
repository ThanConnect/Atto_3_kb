Replacement of rear assembly of side wall

Warning

To avoid repair accidents, the Operation specifications and requirements  shall be observed during operation.

Caution

  • Before operation, please carefully read and observe site safety and vehicle protection. For details, please refer to Site safety and vehicle protection 

  • During operation, please take safety protection measures as required. For details of safety protection, please refer to Sheet metal protection 

Reminder

  • The removal and installation methods of the left one are similar to those of the right one. Take the left one as an example.

  • The circular icon in the illustration in this document does not indicate the specific quantity of welding spots, but only indicates the approximate positions of welding spots. The specific quantity shall be subject to the actual vehicle.

Operation tools and auxiliary materials

Reminder

The tools and auxiliary materials described in this document are recommended, which shall be subject to the actual situation.

  • Cutting plates: pneumatic saw. Refer to Cutting tools 

  • Removal of welding spots/drilling: spot welding removal drill, special drill bit. Refer to Welding spot removal tool 

  • Cleaning/trimming flats: abrasive wheel grinder. Refer to Grinding tools 

  • Cleaning: degreaser, tack cloth. Refer to Grinding tools 

  • Welding: resistance spot welder, gas shielded welder. Refer to Welding tools 

  • Anti-corrosion:

    • Tool: spray gun. Refer to Anti-corrosion tools 

    • Auxiliary materials: Refer to Anti-corrosion materials 

      • Anti-corrosion of joint surface: zinc powder conductive primer

      • Anti-corrosion of welding surface: anti-rust primer

Removal

Before removing the C-pillar reinforcement plate, remove part of the door sill reinforcement plate.

Step I: Remove part of the door sill reinforcement plate.

  1. Remove the rear cross beam assembly of roof cover. Refer to Replacement of front/rear cross beam assembly of roof cover 

  2. Remove the side wall outer panel (C-pillar). Refer to Replacement of side wall outer panel (C-pillar) 

    Reminder

    Refer to Scenario III in side wall outer panel (C-pillar) for operation

  3. After cutting the marked area with an abrasive wheel grinder, remove the welding spots formed by continuous seam welding.

    Caution

    Carefully cut only 1 layer of metal parts to avoid damage to the reinforcement.

    1. Cut the marked area.

    2. Remove the welding spots formed by continuous seam welding.

  4. Remove the welding spots with an 8 mm special drill bit. After taking out part of the door sill reinforcement plate, remove burrs on the cutting edge of the vehicle body panel.

    1. Remove 2 layers of welding spots.

    2. Remove 1 layer of welding spots.

Step II: Remove the rear assembly of side wall.

To avoid repeating the step, it is necessary to remove the taillight holder assembly, outer panel of rear gutter channel and rear assembly of side wall together.

  1. Remove the welding spots with an 8 mm special drill bit.

    1. Remove 2 layers of welding spots.

    2. Remove 3 layers of welding spots.

    3. Remove 1 layer of welding spots.

  2. Remove 1 layer of welding spots with an 8 mm special drill bit.

  3. Remove the welding spots.

    1. Remove the welding spots formed by plug welding and continuous seam welding with an abrasive wheel grinder.

    2. Remove 1 layer of welding spots with an 8 mm special drill bit.

  4. Remove the welding spots formed by plug welding and continuous seam welding with an abrasive wheel grinder, and take out the rear assembly of side wall.

    Caution

    This step is difficult to be performed and requires the cooperation of many people.

Installation

Caution

  • During installation of components, the number of welding spots shall be increased by about 30% on the basis of the original number.

  • During welding, the positions of original welding spots shall be avoided.

  • During welding, trial welding shall be carried out to confirm that the machine is properly adjusted. Distance between welding spots = panel thickness * 10 + 10 mm. For welding methods, refer to Resistance spot welding  and Gas shielded welding 

  • To reduce welding slag splashing, the zinc powder conductive primer at the hole position can be removed with steel wire before metal active gas welding.

  • It is recommended to use the tack cloth and degreasing cleaner to clean the joint surfaces.

Step I: Prepare the component for installation.

  1. Prepare the rear assembly of side wall to be installed.

    1. Order the rear assembly of side wall, and make holes with a diameter of 8 mm at the welding position where the new part will be installed.

    2. Grind the joint surface with an abrasive wheel grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

    3. Remove burrs in the circular hole.

    4. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the joint surface.

  2. Order the taillight holder assembly and install it to the rear assembly of side wall.

    1. Grind the joint surface with an abrasive wheel grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

    2. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the mounting surface.

    3. Apply two-component structural sealant to the joint surface.

    4. Fix the taillight holder assembly in place with the locking pliers, and then weld it by resistance welding.

  3. Prepare part of the door sill reinforcement plate to be installed.

    • Finish the joint surface of the component smooth and flat with an abrasive wheel grinder.

Step II: Install the rear assembly of side wall.

  1. Fix the prepared rear assembly of side wall to the vehicle body and measure whether the data of Rear door  and Rear compartment  is correct. Refer to How to measure and align vehicle body 

  2. Handle the joint surface.

    1. Remove the residue on the joint surface with an abrasive wheel grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

    2. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the mounting surface.

    3. Apply two-component structural sealant to the joint surface.

    4. Spray the joint surface with zinc powder conductive primer.

      Caution

      Do not spray it too thick to avoid affecting the welding quality.

  3. Fix the C-pillar reinforcement plate to its original position with the locking pliers, and then weld it.

    1. Use resistance welding.

    2. Use metal active gas welding.

    3. Use metal active gas welding.

  4. Perform welding by continuous seam welding and plug welding.

    1. Perform plug welding by metal active gas welding.

    2. Use continuous seam welding.

  5. Carry out anti-corrosion treatment on the welded parts. Refer to Anti-corrosion treatment 

    1. Grind the parts that have been welded by metal active gas welding to make them smooth and flat.

    2. Remove stains on the surface of components with tack cloth and degreasing cleaner.

    3. Apply anti-rust primer to prevent corrosion on the surface of components.

  6. Re-confirm and check whether the size data of Rear door  and Rear compartment  is correct.

    Reminder

    • If the data is abnormal, refer to How to measure and align vehicle body  for repairing the vehicle body

    • If the data cannot be repaired by correction, the component may need to be reinstalled.

Step III: Install part of the door sill reinforcement plate.

  1. Handle the joint surface.

    1. Remove the residue on the joint surface with an abrasive wheel grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

    2. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the mounting surface.

    3. Spray the joint surface with zinc powder conductive primer.

      Caution

      Do not spray it too thick to avoid affecting the welding quality.

  2. Fix the door sill reinforcement plate to its original position with the locking pliers, and then weld it.

    1. Use metal active gas welding.

    2. Use continuous seam welding.

  3. Carry out anti-corrosion treatment on the welded parts. Refer to Anti-corrosion treatment 

    1. Grind the parts that have been welded by metal active gas welding to make them smooth and flat.

    2. Remove stains on the surface of components with tack cloth and degreasing cleaner.

    3. Apply anti-rust primer to prevent corrosion on the surface of components.

  4. Carry out sealing after operation.

    1. Clean the area to be coated with sealant with tack cloth and degreasing cleaner to ensure that this area is free of sundries, oil stains and water stains.

    2. Apply sealant to the finished metal joints.

  5. Install the outer panel of rear gutter channel. Refer to Replacement of outer panel of rear gutter channel 

    Caution

    Install the outer panel of rear gutter channel as a whole, and ignore the cutting operation for reference.

  6. Install the side wall outer panel (C-pillar). Refer to Replacement of side wall outer panel (C-pillar) 

    Reminder

    Refer to Scenario III in side wall outer panel (C-pillar) for operation

  7. Install the rear cross beam assembly of roof cover. Refer to Replacement of front/rear cross beam assembly of roof cover 

  8. After the vehicle body components are installed, conduct a waterproof test:

    Caution

    • Cure the sealant before the waterproof test.

    • Before the waterproof test, remove the rear left/rear right wheel according to the actual scenario after confirming that the jack is firmly fixed.

    • After the waterproof test, dry and check the tested parts, and carry out anti-corrosion treatment in case of a risk of rust.

    • No interior trims need to be installed before the end of waterproof test.

    • If there is a problem, find the leaking part and deal it with according to the actual situation.
