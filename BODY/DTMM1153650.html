Common problems and solutions

Reminder

This document summarizes the common problems and solutions that may be encountered during polishing.

Low glossiness

  • Cause:

    • Surface polishing before sufficient drying of paint coating.

    • Use of inferior polishing wax.

  • Solution:

    • Dry the surface thoroughly and then polish it. Refer to Performing polishing 

    • Select an appropriate polishing material according to the type of paint.

Polishing marks

  • Cause: Surface polishing before sufficient drying

  • Solution: Wait for sufficient drying before repolishing; if the operation is ineffective, repaint the surface. Refer to:

    • Solvent-based plain paint 

    • Solvent-based metallic paint 

    • Spraying of plastic parts 

Sandpaper marks

  • Cause: Insufficient rough polishing or too deep sandpaper marks

  • Solution: Re-select fine polishing sandpaper to grind the defective area before polishing (Performing polishing ).

Rough texture

  • Cause: Insufficient or excessive polishing

  • Solution:

    • Insufficient polishing: Carry out further polishing. Refer to Performing polishing 

    • Excessive polishing: Repaint. Refer to:

      • Solvent-based plain paint 

      • Solvent-based metallic paint 

      • Spraying of plastic parts 

Exposure due to polishing

  • Cause: Excessive polishing

  • Common part: Vehicle body contour feature line.

  • Solution: Repaint. Refer to:

    • Solvent-based plain paint 

    • Solvent-based metallic paint 

    • Spraying of plastic parts 

Coating delamination

  • Cause:

    • Excessive polishing of coating surface in the blend area, resulting in coating cracking.

    • The polishing machine moves from the undamaged surface to the repainted surface during polishing.

  • Solution: Repaint. Refer to:

    • Solvent-based plain paint 

    • Solvent-based metallic paint 

    • Spraying of plastic parts
