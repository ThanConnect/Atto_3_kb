Cutting tools

Reminder

Tools in this document are general-purpose ones available on the market and can be purchased as required.

Pneumatic saw

  • Purpose: depending on the different saw blades assembled:

    • 32-tooth saw blade: used for cutting single-layer or multi-layer steel panels with a thickness ≤ 1 mm

    • 24-tooth saw blade: used for cutting steel panels with a thickness ≤ 4 mm

    • 18-tooth saw blade: used for curved cutting of softer steel panels

    • 14-tooth saw blade: used for cutting aluminum or plastic components

Pneumatic shear

  • Purpose: used for rough cutting of vehicle body's outer panel.

  • Characteristic:

    • Fast cutting speed, suitable for cutting thin panels.

    • No cutting chips or sparks will be generated.

    • Different cutting operations can be completed with different accessories.

  • Disadvantage: large deformation of sheet metal during cutting.

Steel panel shear

  • Purpose: used for precise or fine cutting.

  • Operation instructions: Select an appropriate steel panel shear for cutting according to the shape of the tool.

    • Straight steel shear: It is used for cutting straight lines and large arc curves.

    • Semi-bending shear: It is used for cutting outer arc curves.

    • Large bending shear: It is used for cutting inner circular curves.

Abrasive wheel grinder

  • Purpose: It can be used for cutting or grinding, with different accessories and functions.

    • Equipped with abrasive grinding wheel: It can be used to grind welding spots and welds continuously welded by the paint machine.

    • Equipped with cutting grinding wheel: It can be used to cut the vehicle body panels.

      Danger

      To prevent the grinding wheel from breaking, the cutting force shall not be too large.

Plasma cutting machine

  • Cutting principle: The gas is ionized into an ionic state, and the molten metal is cut by blowing off with the compressed gas ejected from the cutting gun.

  • Operation instructions:

    • Contact cutting: It is generally suitable for cutting thin panels. The method is to contact the plasma cutting gun nozzle with the cutting panel, ignite the plasma arc, and cut the panel forward at a constant speed.

    • Non-contact cutting: A certain gap is reserved between the nozzle and the panel. The cutting machine moves at a constant speed and cuts through the panel. The gap is generally 3-5 mm.
