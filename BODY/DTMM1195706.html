Welding tools

Reminder

Tools in this document are general-purpose ones available on the market and can be purchased as required.

Resistance spot welder

  • Purpose: It is used to weld metal plates by using low voltage and high current to melt the pressed part through resistance heating and maintaining the applied pressure.

  • Characteristic:

    • When it is necessary to join the vehicle body panels, resistance spot welding is the most reliable method, which is the same as the original manufacturing process.

    • Filler metals such as welding wires and welding rods, as well as welding materials such as oxygen, acetylene and hydrogen are not required, so the welding cost is low.

    • There will be no residual internal stress and deformation at the welding position.

    • The heat penetrated into the steel panel during welding is low, the thermal deformation is small, and the unwelded areas will not harden.

    • It has high productivity and is free of noise and harmful gases, but sometimes sparks splashing may occur, which calls for isolation.

  • How to use:

    • Apply anti-rust primer after sheet metal repair.

    • Remove water, grease, and other affected substances.

    • Spray or brush on exposed metal panels.

    • During use, select the appropriate electrode arm according to the welding position of the vehicle body. The longer the electrode arm, the larger the operating space and the greater the control difficulty.

Gas shielded welder

  • Introduction: The arc welding that uses the mixture of carbon dioxide and argon as the shielding gas, which protects the arc and the welding area against environmental influence, is called carbon dioxide gas shielded arc welding, or gas shielded welding for short.

  • Characteristic:

    • The welding operation is easy, without or with little welding slag, and there is basically no need for slag removal after welding.

    • With the compression of the protective gas flow, the arc heat is concentrated; the welding speed is faster; the weld pool is smaller; the heat-affected zone is narrow; the weldment deformation after welding is small.

    • It can be used to weld magnesium, aluminum, and their alloys with strong chemical activity and easy formation of high melting point oxide film.

    • It can be used to weld sheets.

    • When working outdoors, windshield devices shall be set up; otherwise, the effect of gas shielded welding is not good or even poor.

    • The light radiation of the arc is strong.

    • The welding equipment is relatively complex and more expensive than the shielded metal arc welding equipment.

  • Operation instructions:

    • The welding wire is sent out through the electric motor.

    • The visibility of gas shielded arc welding and weld pool is good, and the welding parameters can be adjusted according to the conditions of the weld pool during welding.

    • The tightness of the pinch roller of the wire feed motor shall be adjusted moderately.

    • Choose different grooves for the pinch roller according to the size of welding wire.

Conduit

Caution

Never bend the conduit during welding, otherwise, the uniform output of welding wire may be affected.

  • Introduction: conduit metal spiral construction

  • Purpose: It can ensure smooth delivery of the welding wire to the nozzle position.

Conductive nozzle and welding gun nozzle

Caution

Once the welding wire is heated, it will expand. The diameter of the contact nozzle will be slightly larger than that of the welding wire. However, if the gap is too large, it will lead to wire output and unstable arc.

  • Purpose: As one end of the electrode, the conductive nozzle is used to conduct current to the welding wire, so the inner wall of the conductive nozzle must be kept in contact with the welding wire.

  • Operation instructions:

    • The inside of the welding gun nozzle must be kept clean and free from excessive welding slag.

    • Conductive nozzles are available in three models: 0.6 mm, 0.8 mm, and 1.0 mm. For replacement, it is important to choose a model that matches with the welding wire.
