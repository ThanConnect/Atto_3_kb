Specifications and requirements

Warning message

Warning

  • Before operation, master the positions and other important information of the high-voltage system and circuits of the corresponding vehicle.

  • To keep the electrical system safe, disconnect the high-voltage system and 12 V battery before repair.

  • When welding the vehicle body, properly lay fire blankets in the corresponding areas.

  • Avoid direct contact with high-voltage components and wiring harnesses in the repair area, and operate according to the safety requirements of the high-voltage system.

  • Do not heat the bonding area above 180℃; otherwise, toxic gas will be emitted.

  • Before welding, confirm that there are no flammable and explosive liquid substances in the operation area, and effective fire extinguishing equipment is placed within one meter of the operation area.

  • Evaluate whether the high heat during operation affects the high-voltage battery. If so, remove the battery before operation.

Removal of welding spots

  • Use an 8 mm spotting drill bit, and do not damage the retained parts of vehicle body.

Detachment from bonding area

  • The heating temperature shall not exceed 180°C.

Resistance welding

  • Pre-treatment: Grind (the paint layer 30 mm around the welding spot to the galvanized layer or bare metal) and clean four sides of the welding area, and brush or spray zinc powder conductive primer on the joint surface.

  • Requirements:

    • Before welding, trial welding shall be carried out to confirm that the machine is properly adjusted. Distance between welding spots = panel thickness * 10 + 10 mm. The number of welding spots shall be increased by about 30% on the basis of the original number. When welding two layers, it is necessary to avoid the original welding spots for welding; when welding three-layer plates, it is necessary to carry out welding at the original welding spots.

  • Inspection requirements: Select one-tenth of the welding spots in the continuous seam welding area for non-destructive test.

Adhesive bonding requirements

  • Pre-treatment: Grind (the paint layer 30 mm around the welding spot to the galvanized layer or bare metal) and clean four sides of the welding area.

  • Requirements: If the ambient temperature is 15-23 °C, fix it within 30 minutes and do not remove it within 12 hours.

  • Process: Grind the joint surface with P150 sandpaper -> clean it with detergent -> wait for drying (about 5 minutes) -> apply sealant.

Plug welding

  • Pre-treatment: Grind (the paint layer 30 mm around the welding spot to the galvanized layer or bare metal) and clean four sides of the welding area, and brush or spray zinc powder conductive primer on the joint surface.

  • Requirements: The hole diameter of the outer panel is 6 mm, and that of the structural part is 8 mm. The distance between holes is 30 mm-40 mm.

    Caution

    To reduce welding slag splashing, the zinc powder conductive primer at the hole position can be removed with steel wire before welding.

  • Anti-corrosion treatment: Grind the surface of the welding spot to a flat one, and wax the cavity inside for anti-corrosion. Refer to Anti-corrosion treatment 

Butt welding

  • Pre-treatment: Grind (the paint layer 30 mm around the welding spot to the galvanized layer or bare metal) and clean welds or the paint layer on the back of the welding spot, so as to prevent dirt from entering the welding pool through the weld root.

  • Requirement: When carbon dioxide gas shielded welding is used, the welding gap shall be less than 1 mm. The larger the welding gap, the lower the connection strength.

  • Reinforcement requirements: When the interface is welded, a reinforcement with a width of 40-60 mm shall be fabricated. Before welding of the reinforcement, it shall be ground to bare metal and then coated with zinc powder conductive primer.

  • Post-treatment:

    • When grinding welds, avoid the base material other than the welds.

    • Clean all welds, welding spots and burned paint with a wire brush.

    • Seal original welding areas that have been removed after application of primer.

    • Protect the internal cavity with cavity anti-corrosion wax. Refer to Anti-corrosion treatment 

    • Replace the sound insulation material.
