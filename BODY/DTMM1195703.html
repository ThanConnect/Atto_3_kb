Anti-corrosion tools

Reminder

Tools in this document are general-purpose ones and can be purchased as required.

Spray gun

  • Purpose: It is used to spray anti-corrosive paint.

  • Characteristics:

    • It is suitable for large-area spraying operations.

    • The spraying effect is relatively uniform.

Glue gun

Caution

The glue gun has pneumatic, manual and electric types. For a pneumatic or electric glue gun, the pressure shall not be too large, which shall be gradually adjusted from small to large.

  • Purpose: As a special tool for applying cylindrical glue, it is used to apply various sheet metal sealants.

Cavity anti-corrosive spray gun

Caution

The spray gun with a hose has a large spraying angle, which can be changed to cover the surface of the inner cavity in almost all positions.

  • Purpose:

    • Corrosion and rust prevention treatment shall be carried out for inaccessible positions.

    • It is used for spraying wax oil based internal rust inhibitor.

    • It is used for the anti-corrosion treatment of the inner cavities of door pillars and door sills.

    • It is used for other damaged areas of sheet metal repair (where painting cannot be carried out inside).

Brush

Caution

Soak and wash after use.

  • Purpose: It is used to apply liquid primer and sealant.
