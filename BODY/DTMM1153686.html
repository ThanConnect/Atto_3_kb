Replacement of front connecting plate assembly of door sill inner panel

Warning

To avoid repair accidents, the Operation specifications and requirements  shall be observed during operation.

Caution

  • Before operation, please carefully read and observe site safety and vehicle protection. For details, please refer to Site safety and vehicle protection 

  • During operation, please take safety protection measures as required. For details of safety protection, please refer to Sheet metal protection 

Reminder

  • The removal and installation methods of the left one are similar to those of the right one. Take the left one as an example.

  • The circular icon in the illustration in this document does not indicate the specific quantity of welding spots, but only indicates the approximate positions of welding spots. The specific quantity shall be subject to the actual vehicle.

  • There are various process symbols in the technical illustrations in this document. To understand their meanings, please refer to Description of diagram 

Operation tools and auxiliary materials

Reminder

The tools and auxiliary materials described in this document are recommended, which shall be subject to the actual situation.

  • Cutting plates: abrasive wheel grinder. Refer to Cutting tools 

  • Removal of welding spots: spot welding removal drill, special drill bit. Refer to Welding spot removal tool 

  • Cleaning/trimming flats: abrasive wheel grinder. Refer to Grinding tools 

  • Cleaning: degreaser, tack cloth. Refer to Grinding tools 

  • Welding: resistance spot welder, gas shielded welder. Refer to Welding tools 

  • Anti-corrosion:

    • Tool: spray gun. Refer to Anti-corrosion tools 

    • Auxiliary materials: Refer to Anti-corrosion materials 

      • Anti-corrosion of joint surface: zinc powder conductive primer

      • Anti-corrosion of welding surface: anti-rust primer

Removal

Due to structural reasons, during removal of the front connecting plate assembly of door sill inner panel, the rear section of front longitudinal beam cover panel shall be removed together with part of the door sill inner panel reinforcement plate.

  1. Remove the lower section of A-pillar inner panel. Refer to Replacement of lower section of A-pillar inner panel 

  2. Cut the marked area with a pneumatic saw.

  3. Remove the welding spots with an 8 mm special drill bit, and take out the front connecting plate assembly of door sill.

    Reminder

    Before removing the welding spot, remove the sealant on its surface.

    1. Remove 2 layers of welding spots.

    2. Remove 3 layers of welding spots.

    3. Remove 1 layer of welding spots.

Installation

Caution

  • During installation of components, the number of welding spots shall be increased by about 30% on the basis of the original number.

  • During welding, the positions of original welding spots shall be avoided.

  • During welding, trial welding shall be carried out to confirm that the machine is properly adjusted. Distance between welding spots = panel thickness * 10 + 10 mm. For welding methods, refer to Resistance spot welding  and Gas shielded welding 

  • To reduce welding slag splashing, the zinc powder conductive primer at the hole position can be removed with steel wire before metal active gas welding.

  • It is recommended to use the tack cloth and degreasing cleaner to clean the joint surfaces.

Step I: Prepare the component for installation.

  1. Order spare parts and merge the front connecting plate assembly of door sill inner panel with the rear section of front longitudinal beam cover panel.

    1. Order the rear section assembly of front longitudinal beam and remove the rear section of front longitudinal beam cover panel.

      1. Mark, cut and remove the required components by comparing with the cutting area of actual vehicle.

      2. Remove the welding spots with an 8 mm special drill bit, and take out the rear section of front longitudinal beam cover panel.

    2. Order the front connecting plate assembly of door sill and weld it with the rear section of front longitudinal beam cover panel.

      1. Grind the joint surface with an abrasive wheel grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

      2. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the joint surface.

        Reminder

        Recommended auxiliary materials: tack cloth and degreasing cleaner.

      3. Apply two-component structural sealant to the joint surface.

      4. Fix the front connecting plate assembly of door sill and the front longitudinal beam cover panel in place with the locking pliers, and then weld them by resistance welding.

        Caution

        After the components are merged, clean up the two-component structural sealant on the edge of the components.

      5. Make holes (diameter: 8 mm) with a special drill bit at the welding position of the new component.

      6. Remove burrs in the circular hole.

  2. Prepare the door sill inner panel reinforcement plate (partial).

    Reminder

    This is a simple step and will not be repeated here.

    1. Remove the inner panel reinforcement plate from the old part with an 8 mm special drill bit.

    2. Remove the residue on the joint surface with a grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

Step II: Install the front connecting plate assembly of door sill inner panel and the rear section of front longitudinal beam cover panel.

  1. Handle the joint surface.

    1. Remove the residue on the joint surface with a grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

    2. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the joint surface.

      Reminder

      Recommended auxiliary materials: tack cloth and degreasing cleaner.

    3. Spray the joint surface with zinc powder conductive primer.

      Caution

      Do not spray it too thick to avoid affecting the welding quality.

  2. Fix the component to be installed to its original position with the locking pliers, and then weld it.

    1. Use metal active gas welding.

    2. Use resistance welding.

    3. Use continuous seam welding.

Step III: Install the door sill inner panel reinforcement plate (partial).

  1. Handle the joint surface.

    1. Remove the residue on the joint surface with a grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

    2. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the joint surface.

      Reminder

      Recommended auxiliary materials: tack cloth and degreasing cleaner.

    3. Spray the joint surface with zinc powder conductive primer.

      Caution

      Do not spray it too thick to avoid affecting the welding quality.

  2. Fix the front longitudinal beam inner panel of front hubcap to its original position with the locking pliers, and then weld it.

    1. Use resistance welding.

    2. Use continuous seam welding.

  3. Carry out anti-corrosion treatment on the welded parts. Refer to Anti-corrosion treatment 

    1. Grind the parts that have been welded by metal active gas welding to make them smooth and flat.

    2. Remove stains on the surface of components with tack cloth and degreasing cleaner.

    3. Apply anti-rust primer to prevent corrosion on the surface of components.

  4. Install the lower section of A-pillar inner panel. Refer to Replacement of lower section of A-pillar inner panel 

  5. After the vehicle body components are installed, conduct a waterproof test:

    Caution

    • Cure the sealant before the waterproof test.

    • After the waterproof test, dry and check the tested parts, and carry out anti-corrosion treatment in case of a risk of rust.

    • No interior trims need to be installed before the end of waterproof test.

    • If there is a problem, find the leaking part and deal it with according to the actual situation.

  6. Check whether the size data of vehicle body is correct:

    • Check the front compartment data. Refer to Front compartment 

    • Check the floor data. Refer to Underbody 

    Reminder

    • If the data is abnormal, refer to How to measure and align vehicle body  for repairing the vehicle body

    • If the data cannot be repaired by correction, the component may need to be reinstalled.
