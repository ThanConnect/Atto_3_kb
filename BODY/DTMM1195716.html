Masking material

Reminder

This document introduces the auxiliary materials involved in masking. The specific brands and models of auxiliary materials shall be subject to the actual situation.

Masking paper

  • Purpose: It is used to mask areas where panels do not need to be sprayed.

  • Characteristic: The solvent cannot penetrate. The masking paper will not generate dust, and the paint adhering to the paper will not fall off.

Masking tape

  • Purpose: It is used to fix the masking paper and film and to mask the boundary.

Masking film

  • Purpose: It is used to cover a larger area than masking paper, improve the masking efficiency and reduce costs.

  • Characteristic: It has static electricity, which is convenient to be adsorbed on the vehicle body and can absorb paint dust.

Window edge masking tape

  • Purpose: It is used to mask the sealing strip or glass protective strip.

  • Characteristic: It can be effectively inserted into the rubber strip for reverse masking to avoid paint edges.

Gap tape

  • Purpose: It is used for the gaps around the hood, doors and trunk cover board to prevent paint mist from passing through gaps and polluting the interior of panels.
