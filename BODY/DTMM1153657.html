Performing masking

Caution

  • During operation, please take safety protection measures as required. For details of safety protection, please refer to Spraying protection 

  • Before operation, please carefully read and observe site safety and vehicle protection. For details, please refer to Site safety and vehicle protection 

Reminder

This document takes the masking when coating rear doors and fenders and the masking when coating front doors as examples to guide you to perform masking.

Masking when coating rear doors and fenders

  1. Clean the front panel gaps and assembly parts in the masking area with a degreasing cleaner to ensure that the corresponding area is free of dust, moisture, and oil stains.

  2. Paste the inner side of the panel: paste the masking paper along the inner side edge of the rear fender panel to avoid the panel being contaminated by paint during spraying.

    Reminder

    For the inner area of the door, it is necessary to paste the masking paper along the sealing strip.

  3. Paste the trim strip: Center fold the masking tape and paste it to the edge of the rear fender on the upper side of the door, then close the door, and paste the remaining tape to the door frame, leaving a suitable gap at the edge of the panel.

  4. Paste the masking paper: paste the masking paper along the edges of the panel respectively. Be careful when pasting to ensure that the masked surface is smooth and avoid any omission, excessive pasting, steps, and wrinkles.

  5. Paste the masking film: Cut a suitable length of masking film and paste it to the vehicle body part.

    Reminder

    After the vehicle is moved to the spray booth, mask the tires, windshields, or other parts.

  6. Mask the blend area: After degreasing and cleaning, mask the blend area with reverse masking method.

Masking when coating front doors

  1. Clean the front panel gaps and assembly parts in the masking area with a degreasing cleaner to ensure that the corresponding area is free of dust, moisture, and oil stains.

  2. Paste doors: Paste the edge of the panel, the upper trim cover on the fender, and the lower wheel arch area to prevent paint mist from contaminating the inner side during spraying.

  3. Paste the masking paper:

    • Paste the masking paper slightly longer than the edge of the panel to the adjacent panel along the edge of the panel, and avoid wrinkles during pasting.

    • Finally, paste the vehicle body trim strip and make sure to reserve suitable gap during pasting.

  4. Paste the masking film: Move the vehicle to the spray booth, paste and cut the masking film reasonably according to the pasting condition of the masking paper to ensure that there is no omission.

  5. Paste the vehicle body trim strips: Paste the vehicle body trim strips and reserve appropriate gaps, to prevent the vehicle body trim strips from being attached by paint during spraying or avoid sticking the adhesive tape together with paint after spraying.

    Caution

    Reserve a gap with the same coating thickness between the steel panel surface and the masking tape during pasting.

  6. Paste the door handle hole. Refer to Masking methods and skills 

  7. Check the masking quality to avoid paint mist contaminating the vehicle body during spraying.
