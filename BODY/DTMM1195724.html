Vehicle body alignment

Reminder

Tools in this document are general-purpose ones available on the market and can be purchased as required.

If the outer panel and inner structure are deformed due to severe impact of the vehicle, it is necessary to use tools and equipment for vehicle body alignment and measurement to draw, correct, measure, cut, joint or replace the affected vehicle so as to restore to its original appearance and quality.

Lift-type alignment rack

  • Purpose: It can measure multiple three-dimensional size data between the upper part, lower part, and components of the vehicle body.

  • Characteristic:

    • It is easy and simple to operate, usually in conjunction with an electronic measuring system.

    • Bluetooth is available for output and recording of the measured dimensions in time, so as to accurately judge the change of data.

    • It has a large database to store the vehicle body data of various models, and accurate measured data, with very small error.

Removable alignment rack

  • Characteristic:

    • It can be moved as required to repair the vehicle.

    • It is convenient for alignment and repair of the vehicle body side damage.

Small damage repair and alignment rack

It can also be called the ground trigram bench, which are buried below the ground and have a surface flush with the ground.

  • Characteristic: With the movable pull tower, it is convenient and quick to repair the affected vehicle with small damage.

Platform-type alignment rack

  • Characteristic: It is convenient and quick to install the underbody fixtures in multiple positions at the same time.

Ground-cage alignment rack

  • Characteristic: It is available for all-round alignment and drawing for the affected vehicle.

Pull tower

  • Characteristic:

    • It is fixed on the side of the alignment rack.

    • It can draw the vehicle body with damage and deformation at different positions and angles.

Fixture

  • Purpose: It is used to fix the bottom of the middle section of the vehicle body, providing a fixed support for drawing alignment.

  • Instructions for use:

    • There are usually four fixtures.

    • Check the clearance between the vehicle body and fixture at any time during drawing alignment.

    • In case of any abnormality, reinstall and fix it and tighten the bolts.

Vehicle body chassis fixing bracket

  • Purpose: It is used for the vehicle body fixing and height adjustment.

  • Characteristic: It can adjust the positions of front, rear, left, and right supports for flexible use.

  • Instructions for use: It is used in conjunction with fixtures.

Drawing fixture

  • Note: It is necessary for drawing alignment.

  • Instructions for use: According to different damages and positions, select the correct fixture before operation and use correctly:

    • Edge-clamped drawing fixture: It is mainly used for fixing at the edge of vehicle body.

    • Flat drawing fixture: It is mainly used for fixing the barrier-free edge of the vehicle body.

    • Hook-type drawing fixture: It is mainly used for fixing the edge of vehicle body.

    • Two-way drawing fixture: With two drawing chains installed, it is mainly used to draw at two angles simultaneously.

Safety cable

Danger

It must be installed before drawing alignment to prevent potential safety hazards caused by fixture slipping and chain breakage during drawing.

  • Purpose: It is used to prevent personal injury caused by fixture falling off or breakage during vehicle body alignment.
