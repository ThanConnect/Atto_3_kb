Cutting principle of metal parts

  • Cutting principles:

    • The cutting position shall be set at the area without reinforcement on the inner side as possible.

    • There should be no stress concentration area (fold design) at the cutting position.

    • The welding joint area at the cutting position is small, which is convenient for spraying.

    • The cutting and welding position shall be selected in a small area so that the thermal deformation of panels is small due to the small heat generated by welding, which is conducive to improving the efficiency of spraying process.

    • The cutting and welding area shall be selected at a position covered by the exterior spare parts to reduce the time for fine treatment after welding at this position and improve the operation efficiency (such as the installation positions of rear fender quarter window and rear trim strip of quarter window of Song model).

    • Select a position with few spare parts installed, or a position with installed spare parts that cover the welding joint area.

  • Principle of non-cutting:

    • Key reinforcements composed of hot-formed steel materials and other materials (such as A-pillar, B-pillar, front hubcap assembly) shall not be cut: If there is a reinforced structural part inside the cutting panel, it will not only affect the normal cutting stroke of the cutting tool, but also have the risk of damaging the inner reinforced structural part during cutting.

    • The fold design area cannot be cut: After the cutting and welding in the fold design area are completed, when a frontal collision occurs again at this part of the vehicle, the fold design area will not crumple or its crumple function will decrease, which will affect the absorption of impact energy and thus reduce the safety performance of the vehicle body.

    • The front longitudinal beam of the integral body not only ensures the torsional rigidity of the frame and bears longitudinal load, but also supports the main components of the vehicle. Therefore, it must be replaced in the form of an assembly provided as a spare part in case of bending deformation after a collision. And cutting replacement is not allowed.

  • For the identification of vehicle body steel material and strength, refer to Steel materials and strength
