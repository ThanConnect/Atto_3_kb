Removal of welding spots

Reminder

Tools in this document are general-purpose ones available on the market and can be purchased as required.

Belt grinder

  • Purpose: When different grinding abrasive belts are installed, its functions are different:

    • Installation of Cubitron abrasive belt: It can be used to remove welding spots.

    • Installation of abrasive belt:

      • Used for grinding steel panels.

      • Used for grinding the old paint coating.

      • Used for grinding welding spots.

      • Used for grinding narrow spaces.

Spot welding removal drill

  • Purpose: It is used to drill the welding spots of the vehicle body and has the function of fixing the drilling position.

  • Characteristic: Fast operation speed, improving the efficiency of removing steel panel.

  • Disadvantage: It is easily influenced by the operating space during use.

Special drill bit

  • Purpose: It can be used for operation on high-hardness components.

  • Characteristic: It is easily influenced by the operating space during use.

    • Two or three cutting edges.

    • High operation efficiency.

    • Super-hard material.

Pneumatic drill

  • Characteristic:

    • Used in different scenarios according to the specifications of drill bits.

    • Gun-type shape, high operation efficiency.
