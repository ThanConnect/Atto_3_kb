Replacement of upper section of A-pillar reinforcement plate

Warning

To avoid repair accidents, the Operation specifications and requirements  shall be observed during operation.

Caution

  • Before operation, please carefully read and observe site safety and vehicle protection. For details, please refer to Site safety and vehicle protection 

  • During operation, please take safety protection measures as required. For details of safety protection, please refer to Sheet metal protection 

Reminder

  • The removal and installation methods of the left one are similar to those of the right one. Take the left one as an example.

  • The circular icon in the illustration in this document does not indicate the specific quantity of welding spots, but only indicates the approximate positions of welding spots. The specific quantity shall be subject to the actual vehicle.

  • There are various process symbols in the technical illustrations in this document. To understand their meanings, please refer to Description of diagram 

Operation tools and auxiliary materials

Reminder

The tools and auxiliary materials described in this document are recommended, which shall be subject to the actual situation.

  • Cutting plates: pneumatic saw, abrasive wheel cutting machine. Refer to Cutting tools 

  • Removal of welding spots/drilling: spot welding removal drill, special drill bit. Refer to Welding spot removal tool 

  • Cleaning/trimming flats: abrasive wheel grinder. Refer to Grinding tools 

  • Cleaning: degreaser, tack cloth. Refer to Grinding tools 

  • Welding: resistance spot welder, gas shielded welder. Refer to Welding tools 

  • Anti-corrosion:

    • Tool: spray gun. Refer to Anti-corrosion tools 

    • Auxiliary materials: Refer to Anti-corrosion materials 

      • Anti-corrosion of joint surface: zinc powder conductive primer

      • Anti-corrosion of welding surface: anti-rust primer

Removal

To avoid repeated work, remove part of the side wall, upper section of A-pillar reinforcement plate and upper section of A-pillar inner panel together.

Step I: Remove part of the side wall outer panel.

  1. Remove the roof cover assembly. Refer to Replacement of roof cover assembly 

  2. Remove the lower section of A-pillar reinforcement plate. Refer to Replacement of lower section of A-pillar reinforcement plate 

  3. Cut the marked area with a pneumatic saw.

    Caution

    Carefully cut only 1 layer of metal parts to avoid damage to the reinforcement.

  4. Remove 1 layer of welding spots with an 8 mm special drill bit.

  5. After heating the NVH damping tape with a hot air gun, take out part of the side wall outer panel and remove burrs on the cutting edge of the vehicle body panel.

    Caution

    The heating temperature shall not exceed 180°C.

Step II: Remove the upper section of A-pillar reinforcement plate.

  1. Remove 1 layer of welding spots with an 8 mm special drill bit.

  2. Remove the welding spots formed by plug welding with an abrasive wheel grinder.

  3. Remove 1 layer of welding spots with an 8 mm special drill bit.

  4. Take out part of the side wall, upper section of A-pillar reinforcement plate and upper section of A-pillar inner panel together.

    Caution

    This step is difficult to be performed and requires the cooperation of many people.

Installation

Caution

  • During installation of components, the number of welding spots shall be increased by about 30% on the basis of the original number.

  • During welding, the positions of original welding spots shall be avoided.

  • During welding, trial welding shall be carried out to confirm that the machine is properly adjusted. Distance between welding spots = panel thickness * 10 + 10 mm. For welding methods, refer to Resistance spot welding  and Gas shielded welding 

  • To reduce welding slag splashing, the zinc powder conductive primer at the hole position can be removed with steel wire before metal active gas welding.

  • It is recommended to use the tack cloth and degreasing cleaner to clean the joint surfaces.

Step I: Prepare the component for installation.

  1. Prepare the upper section of A-pillar reinforcement plate to be installed.

    1. Order the A-pillar reinforcement plate, and make holes (diameter: 8 mm) with a special drill bit at the welding position of the new component.

    2. Grind the joint surface with an abrasive wheel grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

    3. Remove burrs in the circular hole.

    4. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the joint surface.

  2. Prepare the lower section of A-pillar inner panel to be installed.

    1. Order the lower section of A-pillar inner plate, and make holes (diameter: 8 mm) with a special drill bit at the welding position where the new component will be installed.

    2. Grind the joint surface with an abrasive wheel grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

    3. Remove burrs in the circular hole.

    4. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the joint surface.

  3. Prepare the side wall outer panel and fender mounting bracket to be installed.

    1. Order the front section of side wall outer panel, mark and cut the required components by comparing with the cutting area of actual vehicle, and remove burrs on the cutting edge.

      Caution

      Before cutting, carefully measure and check the dimensions of required materials to ensure normal loading.

    2. Make holes with a diameter of 6 mm at the welding position where the new part will be installed.

      Reminder

      A puncher can be used to drill the component edge, and a drill bit can be used at a position where the puncher cannot be used.

    3. Grind the joint surface with an abrasive wheel grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

    4. Remove burrs in the circular hole.

    5. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the joint surface.

Step II: Install the upper section of A-pillar reinforcement plate.

  1. Fix the prepared rear assembly of side wall to the vehicle body and measure whether the data of Front door and roof  is correct. Refer to How to measure and align vehicle body 

  2. Handle the joint surface.

    1. Remove the residue on the joint surface with an abrasive wheel grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

    2. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the mounting surface.

    3. Apply two-component structural sealant to the joint surface.

    4. Spray the joint surface with zinc powder conductive primer.

      Caution

      Do not spray it too thick to avoid affecting the welding quality.

  3. Install the A-pillar reinforcement plate to its original position.

    Reminder

    The upper section of A-pillar reinforcement plate is integrated with that of A-pillar inner panel.

  4. Fix the upper section of A-pillar reinforcement plate with the locking pliers, and then weld it.

    1. Use resistance welding.

    2. Use metal active gas welding.

    3. Perform plug welding by metal active gas welding.

  5. Carry out anti-corrosion treatment on the welded parts. Refer to Anti-corrosion treatment 

    1. Grind the parts that have been welded by metal active gas welding to make them smooth and flat.

    2. Remove stains on the surface of components with tack cloth and degreasing cleaner.

    3. Apply anti-rust primer to prevent corrosion on the surface of components.

Step III: Install the lower section of A-pillar reinforcement plate.

  1. Handle the joint surface.

    1. Remove the residue on the joint surface with an abrasive wheel grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

    2. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the mounting surface.

    3. Apply two-component structural sealant to the joint surface.

    4. Spray the joint surface with zinc powder conductive primer.

      Caution

      Do not spray it too thick to avoid affecting the welding quality.

  2. Fix the lower section of A-pillar reinforcement plate to its original position with the locking pliers, and then weld it.

    1. Use resistance welding.

    2. Use metal active gas welding.

  3. Weld the welding spots inside the cab by metal active gas welding.

    1. Weld 3 layers.

    2. Weld 2 layers.

  4. Re-confirm and check whether the size data of Front door and roof  is correct.

    Reminder

    • If the data is abnormal, refer to How to measure and align vehicle body  for repairing the vehicle body

    • If the data cannot be repaired by correction, the component may need to be reinstalled.

  5. Carry out anti-corrosion treatment on the welded parts. Refer to Anti-corrosion treatment 

    1. Grind the parts that have been welded by metal active gas welding to make them smooth and flat.

    2. Remove stains on the surface of components with tack cloth and degreasing cleaner.

    3. Apply anti-rust primer to prevent corrosion on the surface of components.

Step III: Install the side wall outer panel.

  1. Handle the joint surface.

    1. Remove the residue on the joint surface with an abrasive wheel grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

    2. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the mounting surface.

    3. Apply two-component structural sealant to the joint surface.

    4. Spray the joint surface with zinc powder conductive primer.

      Caution

      Do not spray it too thick to avoid affecting the welding quality.

  2. Install new NVH damping tape.

  3. Fix the side wall outer panel to its original position with the locking pliers, and then weld it.

    1. Weld 3-layer panels by resistance welding.

    2. Use metal active gas welding.

    3. Use continuous seam welding.

  4. Carry out anti-corrosion treatment on the welded parts. Refer to Anti-corrosion treatment 

    1. Grind the parts that have been welded by metal active gas welding to make them smooth and flat.

      Caution

      After grinding, focus on checking that the roof joint area is smooth and flat without protrusion; otherwise, there will be a risk of poor sealing of the windshield.

    2. Remove stains on the surface of components with tack cloth and degreasing cleaner.

    3. Apply anti-rust primer to prevent corrosion on the surface of components.

  5. Fix the fender lower mounting bracket to its original position with the locking pliers, and then weld it by metal active gas welding.

    Caution

    Spray the joint surface with zinc powder conductive primer before operation.

  6. Carry out anti-corrosion treatment on the welded parts. Refer to Anti-corrosion treatment 

    1. Grind the parts that have been welded by metal active gas welding to make them smooth and flat.

    2. Remove stains on the surface of components with tack cloth and degreasing cleaner.

    3. Apply anti-rust primer to prevent corrosion on the surface of components.

  7. Install the front connecting plate assembly of side wall. Refer to Replacement of front connecting plate assembly of side wall 

  8. Install the roof cover assembly. Refer to Replacement of roof cover assembly 

  9. After the vehicle body components are installed, conduct a waterproof test:

    Caution

    Cure the sealant before the waterproof test.

    • No interior trims need to be installed before the end of waterproof test.

    • If there is a problem, find the leaking part and deal it with according to the actual situation.
