Drying equipment and tools

Reminder

Tools in this document are general-purpose ones available on the market and can be purchased as required.

Spray booth

Caution

  • Do not generate the naked flame in the spray booth, such as smoking.

  • Non-staff workers are prohibited from entering the spray booth. Operators shall take personal protection before entering the booth. Refer to Spraying protection 

  • During spraying operation, close the doors of the spray booth. Never enter and exit the spray booth at will. After the operation, take out all the remaining paints in the spray booth. Never heat up and bake the paints in the spray booth.

  • Requirements: The lighting in the spray booth is required to be above 800 lx. Generally, multiple groups of fluorescent lamps are used on both sides of the roof. In addition, the noise during the operation of the spray booth shall not exceed 80 dB.

  • Maintenance:

    • During maintenance, be sure to turn off the main power switch of the spray booth.

    • Clean and replace the filter cotton (with a general service life of 60-80 h) at the air inlet regularly according to the manufacturer's requirements.

    • Replace the top filter cotton regularly (with a general service life of 350-400 h) according to the manufacturer's requirements.

    • Replace the bottle filter cotton (with a general service life of 80-100 h) regularly according to the manufacturer's requirements.

    • Check the activated carbon filter screen in the activated carbon environmental protection cabinet regularly every quarter. If the surface is covered with paint, replace it in time to ensure smooth ventilation.

    • Check all wires and cables in the spray booth every six months, and replace them if they are aged.

    • Clean the whole spray booth every six months. Check the circulating air valve, air inlet and exhaust fan bearings. Check the smoke exhaust channel of the burner. Clean the sediment in the oil tank. Clean the water-based protective film in the spray booth and perform repainting.

  • Instructions for use:

    • For vehicles repaired with low-temperature paint, the baking temperature is generally 60-80°C.

    • Positive pressure air supply is generally used in the spray booth, and the indoor air supply pressure is generally kept at 4-12 bar higher than outdoors. The positive pressure air supply can ensure no flow of outdoor air into the spray booth and keep the booth clean.

    • The air supply pressure is adjusted by the damper.

    • The air supply rate shall reach 0.2-0.6 m/s during spraying, and the airflow rate is generally 0.05 m/s during heating and baking.

    • The best spraying environment for water-based paint is one with a temperature of 25 °C and a relative humidity of less than 70%.

    • The use of short wave infrared or other thermal radiation equipment will greatly improve the drying speed of the paint film, and has the advantages of thorough drying and consistency inside and outside.

    • Check the circuits of the thermal radiation drying equipment every year and determine whether they need to be replaced. The power circuit of the thermal radiation equipment has the problem of rapid aging.

    • Clean the spray booth once a day. Remove all sundries. Vacuum the floor, wall, grille, etc. to ensure the booth is clean and tidy.

Infrared heating lamp

  • Characteristic: The infrared radiation can penetrate through the paint film surface to the metal surface and then reflect out, so that the bottom layer of the paint absorbs energy first for heating up, and the paint film can be dried from inside to outside.

  • Instructions for use:

    • Generally, the distance between the heating lamp and the workpiece surface usually needs to be greater than 50 cm. Being too close can cause the temperature to rise too quickly, resulting in phenomena such as poly-putty blistering, pinholes, and miliaria-like surface on the panels.

    • Drying speeds vary due to variations in infrared absorption rates caused by different colors of irradiated surfaces. Colors are dependent on reflectance and absorption. For example, dark colors dry faster than light colors.

Blow dryer

  • Purpose: It is used for drying water-based paint.

  • Instructions for use:

    • The blowing direction of the blow dryer must be consistent with the airflow direction of the spray booth.

    • The blowing direction of the blow dryer must be consistent with the airflow direction of the spray booth. Place the blow dryer obliquely above the top of the larger part of the panel.

    • Blowing distance: 30-80 cm
