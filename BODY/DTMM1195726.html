Shaping tool

Reminder

Tools in this document are general-purpose ones available on the market and can be purchased as required.

Sheet metal hammer

  • Purpose: It is used to repair the smooth steel panel surface and basic ridge line.

  • Characteristic:

    • Some hammers have a curved head surface, while others have a horizontal head surface.

    • It usually features a wooden handle.

Needle-nose pliers

  • Purpose: They are used to repair folds or bulges on the panel.

  • Characteristic: They feature different shapes at both ends, with a conical passivated chisel at one end and a circular knocking surface at the other end.

Surface-compressing hammer

  • Purpose: It is used to repair lightly stretched metal parts.

Rubber hammer

  • Purpose: It is used for repair of large-area or relatively soft materials.

  • Characteristic: It is made of soft rubber, with light weight and easy operation.

Bucking block

  • Purpose: It is used together with the sheet metal hammer to repair the plate to its original surface.

  • Usage: After fixing the bucking block to the deformed position, knock the other side with a sheet metal hammer.

Line chisel

  • Purpose: It can repair the ridge lines of different lengths and shapes.

  • Instructions for use:

    • It is used in conjunction with a hammer.

    • It is used for the deformed parts that need to be repaired at edges and corners, and ridge lines.

Crowbar

Caution

During use, pay attention to the position of the supporting point to avoid secondary damage to the vehicle.

  • Purpose: It is used to correct narrow parts that are not easy to reach.

  • Instructions for use:

    • The crowbar, available in different forms, can be selected according to the shapes of steel panels.

Profile gauge

  • Purpose: It is used as an auxiliary inspection tool for repair of the outer panel.

    • For measurement of the panel before repair.

    • For measurement of the panel during repair.

    • For measurement of the panel after repair.

  • Structure: It features a retractable pin structure.

Vehicle body file

Caution

When repairing the panel operated by the meson machine, do not exert force excessively. Otherwise, the panel will be thinner and decreased in strength.

  • Purpose:

    • It is used to smooth out the marks left by tools such as sheet metal hammer, bucking block and leveling blade.

    • It can be used according to the dent of the repaired area in the panel, and also can assist in measurement.

  • Instructions for use: Try to keep an angle of 30~45° during use. Do not exert too much force.

Meson machine

Danger

Check for electric leakage before use.

Caution

  • Remove the battery negative terminal before operation.

  • Carry out trial welding before operation. Otherwise, the panel will be broken down.

Meson machine is used to repair the vehicle body panel. With low voltage and instantaneous large current, it enables the washer to adhere to the metal outer plate of the vehicle body, so as to carry out drawing and shaping.

  • Characteristic:

    • Easy operation

    • With no need to remove too many accessories during operation to improve efficiency

    • Small heat affected area and high repair quality

  • Accessories:

    • Drawing hammer

    • Suction cup

    • Corrugated wires

    • Washer (round or triangular)

    • Drawing hook

    • Carbon rod

    • Copper rod
