Anti-corrosion

Reminder

This document introduces the auxiliary materials involved in the anti-corrosion process. The specific brands and models of auxiliary materials shall be subject to the actual situation.

Anti-rust primer

  • Purpose: It can prevent panels from rusting.

  • Instructions for use: After removing moisture and oil stains on the surface of the panel with special cleaning agent, spray or brush it on the exposed metal panels.

    Caution

    The moisture, oil stains, and stains shall be removed from the surface before application.

Sealant

  • Purpose: It can seal the vehicle body.

  • Advantage: The sealant has a short hardening time and can be delivered to the next process more quickly.

  • Instructions for use:

    1. Use a special cleaning agent to remove moisture and stains on the surface of the panel.

    2. Apply anti-rust primer.

    3. After the anti-rust primer is dried, apply the sealant with the sealant gun.

      Caution

      • After the repair operation, reapply the sealant to the damaged positions of the original sealant.

      • The sealant must be applied after the anti-rust paint on the panels is dried.

Chassis adhesive

  • Purpose: It can prevent the paint surface from being damaged by flying stones on the road and rusted, and has the effects of sound insulation and vibration absorption.

  • Characteristic: The chassis adhesive still has certain softness after drying, and is mainly used at the bottom of door sill or base plate.

  • Instructions for use:

    1. Use a special cleaning agent to remove moisture and stains on the surface of the panel.

    2. Wait until the surface of the panel is completely dry.

    3. Use a special chassis adhesive gun to spray the chassis adhesive.

      Caution

      The chassis adhesive can only be used on the lower part of the sill plate and the bottom plate, and shall not be sprayed on other positions.

Special detergent

Caution

Since the degreaser is generally an organic solvent liquid, personal protection must be done for use.

Purpose:

  • It can remove oil stains on the panel surface.

  • Anti-corrosion cannot be guaranteed without the use of a degreaser.

  • Before carrying out most anti-corrosion work, it is necessary to clean the panel surface with a degreaser.

Cavity anti-corrosion wax

Caution

If the cavity anti-corrosion measures are not strictly carried out, quality problems such as steel panel corrosion and rusting may occur after repair.

Purpose:

  • Corrosion and rust prevention treatment shall be carried out for inaccessible positions.

  • It is used for spraying wax oil based internal rust inhibitor.

  • It is used for the anti-corrosion treatment of the inner cavities of door pillars and door sills.

  • It is used for other damaged areas of sheet metal repair (where painting cannot be carried out inside).

Conductive anti-rust zinc powder paint

  • Introduction: There are self-spraying and brushing types.

  • Purpose: It is used for anti-rust of welding joint surface of panels.
