Polishing tools

Reminder

Tools in this document are general-purpose ones available on the market and can be purchased as required.

Polishing machine

Caution

  • When operating, hold the polishing machine firmly with both hands while keeping the cord or air hose behind you over your shoulders to prevent the cord or air hose from tangling.

  • During the operation, completely press the polishing pad on the workpiece surface before starting the polishing machine. Otherwise, the polishing machine will easily scratch the paint film.

  • Start rotating the polishing machine at a low speed, let the polishing agent adhere to the entire surface, and adjust the speed appropriately.

  • Purpose: It is used to trim the paint film, and divided into pneumatic polishing machine and electric polishing machine:

    • Pneumatic polishing machine: It features a longer service life, making it relatively safer than models with the electrical power as the power source. Ensure stable air source supply during use.

    • Electric polishing machine: It features large driving torque, which can ensure rotation stability under load and will not be affected by insufficiency or absence of air supply.

Polishing pad

Caution

Spread the polishing wax evenly on the polishing pad before polishing.

  • Purpose: It is used to trim the paint film after being installed on the polishing machine. It can be classified into three categories:

    • Wool wheel: It features strong grinding and cutting force and is generally used for rough polishing.

    • Dark polishing pad: It is generally used for fine polishing.

    • Light-colored polishing pad: It is generally used for rough polishing.
