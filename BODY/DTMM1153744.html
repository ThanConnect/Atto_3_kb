Position of foam molded part

The foam molded part is mainly used to control the NVH (noise, vibration and harshness). Vehicles without this part may generate excessive wind noise, tinnitus and other noises during driving.

Caution

After repairing the area involving such material, replace it; otherwise, the possible noise will affect the driving comfort of the vehicle.

Reminder

The positions on the left and right sides are similar, so this document takes the left side as an example.
