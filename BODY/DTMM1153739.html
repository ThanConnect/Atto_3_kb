Steel materials and strength

ATTO 3 is of a high-strength carbody structure, and its vehicle strength is superior to that of products at the same price level. The steel used is as follows:

  • Hot-formed steel accounts for 11%, and the tensile strength of steel is up to 1500 Mpa.

  • High-strength steel and above account for 85%, of which ultra-high strength steel accounts for 55%.

  • Key components, such as front longitudinal beam, rear longitudinal beam, A-pillar, B-pillar, seat cross beam, lower longitudinal beam of floor, door sill longitudinal beam and door sill anti-collision beam, are made of high-strength steel with a tensile strength exceeding 1180 Mpa.

Danger

Do not repair hot-formed steel by cutting or shrinking. If a component made of hot-formed steel is deformed, it needs to be replaced.
