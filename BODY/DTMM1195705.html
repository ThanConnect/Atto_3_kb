Grinding tools

Reminder

Tools in this document are general-purpose ones available on the market and can be purchased as required.

Abrasive wheel grinder

  • Purpose: It can be used for cutting or grinding, with different accessories and functions.

    • Equipped with abrasive grinding wheel: It can be used to grind welding spots and welds continuously welded by the paint machine.

    • Equipped with cutting grinding wheel: It can be used to cut the vehicle body panels.

      Danger

      To prevent the grinding wheel from breaking and causing personal injury, the cutting force shall not be too large.

Adhesive removal grinder

  • Characteristic:

    • The grinding head is made of rubber, and will not damage the panel surface.

    • During operation, no sparks, abrasive dust, toxic gases or fumes shall be generated.

Belt grinder

  • Purpose: When different grinding abrasive belts are installed, its functions are different:

    • Installation of Cubitron abrasive belt: It can be used to remove welding spots.

    • Installation of abrasive belt:

      • Used for grinding steel panels.

      • Used for grinding the old paint coating.

      • Used for grinding welding spots.

      • Used for grinding narrow spaces.
