Primer

Reminder

This document introduces the auxiliary materials involved in application of primer. The specific brands and models of auxiliary materials shall be subject to the actual situation.

The primer coating is the basis of spraying. The primer shall be correctly selected according to the construction procedure and the type of substrates, otherwise the quality of the top coat will be affected by improper use.

Epoxy primer

  • Purpose: Epoxy primer is mainly applied on bare metal. It can rely on the paint film to obtain good adhesion, fill in subtle defects, and also provide anti-corrosion.

  • Operation instructions:

    • The epoxy primer shall be stirred evenly before use. Special curing agent shall be used for the epoxy primer. Attention shall be paid to selecting matching products and mixing them in proportion.

    • It dries slowly, so make sure that a thin layer is applied on the bare metal area during operation.

    • The ideal construction conditions are: 20°C and humidity lower than 80%. If the ambient temperature is lower than 15°C, it is not recommended to adopt the primer due to slow drying.

  • Operation steps: Refer to Primer spraying
