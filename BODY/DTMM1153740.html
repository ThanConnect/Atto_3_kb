Scenario I: Spraying of local parts after repair

Step I: Spray primer layer.

  1. Apply the intermediate paint. Refer to Spraying of intermediate paint 

  2. For preparation, refer to Preliminary preparation 

  3. Panel cleaning: After removing dust and dirt on the clearance of panels and spraying surface by using compressed air with a pressure higher than the spraying pressure, clean the panel with degreaser.

    Caution

    If the panel to be sprayed is a plastic part, remove static electricity with the electrostatic cleaning agent.

  4. Spraying of varnish coating in blend area: After moving the vehicle/panel to the spray booth, apply 1-2 layers in the colored spray area, so that the surface glossiness after spraying reaches 50%.

    Caution

    • Wait for a few seconds after spraying, and judge the glossiness.

    • Control the spraying range. Do not apply too much in one time.

    • When spraying varnish in the blend area, adjust the state of the base color blend area to prevent the paint mist from causing a rough texture.

    • Configuration requirements of spray gun:

      • Diameter of spray gun: 1.3 mm

      • Air pressure (gun tail): 1.5 Bar

      • Paint output: 2.5-3 turns from the fully closed position, and fully open spray pattern.

      • Spraying distance: 15 - 20 cm

      • Overlapping of spraying: 2/3

      • Spraying speed: 60 - 80 cm/s

  5. Mist spraying of primer: Spray a single coating on the intermediate paint to achieve a uniform surface glossiness of 50% immediately after spraying.

    Caution

    • Wait for a few seconds after spraying, and judge the glossiness.

    • Pay attention to the glossiness during spraying. The excessive glossiness will cause paint sagging.

    • Control the spraying range. Do not apply too much in one time.

    • During spraying, check whether there are fish eyes on the surface. If any, increase the pressure of spraying air and spray the surface with dry spraying method to cover them.

    • After spraying, confirm whether there is any defect on the sprayed surface with a fluorescent lamp. If any, refer to FAQs and their solutions  for treatment

    • Configuration requirements of spray gun:

      • Diameter of spray gun: 1.3 mm

      • Air pressure (gun tail): 1 - 1.5 Bar

      • Paint output: 2.5 turns from the fully closed position, and fully open spray pattern.

      • Spraying distance: 15 - 20 cm

      • Overlapping of spraying: 2/3

      • Spraying speed: 60 cm/s

    • Requirements for use of blow dryer:

      • Air pressure: 4 Bar

      • Distance: more than 30 cm

      • Angle: 45°

  6. Colored spraying: Spray primer on the panel surface until the glossiness of the colored spray area is about 50% and there is no obvious blend area. Operation instructions:

    Reminder

    • Wet spraying is required; otherwise, the paint will not adhere properly to the panel surface.

    • Repeat spraying as many times as necessary until the substrate is covered.

    • Insufficient glossiness after application will result in coarse textures.

    • Paint sagging will occur if the coating is too thick at one time.

    • Pinholes will occur if application is carried out while the previous coating has not been dried completely.

    • Configuration requirements of spray gun:

      • Diameter of spray gun: 1.3 mm

      • Air pressure (gun tail): 1 - 1.5 Bar

      • Paint output: 2.0 turns from the fully closed position, and fully open spray pattern.

      • Spraying distance: 10 - 15 cm

      • Overlapping of spraying: 3/4

      • Spraying speed: 60 cm/s

    • Requirements for use of blow dryer:

      • Air pressure: 4 Bar

      • Distance: more than 30 cm

      • Angle: 45°

  7. Removal of spots: Apply a thin coating once or twice with a spray gun to remove coating spots and ensure that the sprayed surface is smooth with a glossiness level of about 50%.

    Reminder

    • After each coating is applied, dry the paint with a blow dryer until the surface glossiness disappears.

    • Insufficient glossiness after application will result in coarse textures.

    • Paint sagging will occur if the coating is too thick at one time.

    • Pinholes will occur if application is carried out while the previous coating has not been dried completely.

    • Configuration requirements of spray gun:

      • Diameter of spray gun: 1.3 mm

      • Air pressure (gun tail): 1.5 Bar

      • Paint output: 2.5-3 turns from the fully closed position, and fully open spray pattern.

      • Spraying distance: 15 - 20 cm

      • Overlapping of spraying: 3/4

      • Spraying speed: 80 cm/s

    • Requirements for use of blow dryer:

      • Air pressure: 4 Bar

      • Distance: more than 30 cm

      • Angle: 45°

Step II: Spray varnish coating.

  1. Mist spraying of varnish: After confirming the application (adhesion by paint mist) area of the base color area, evenly spray a thin layer of varnish to ensure that the varnish application area is slightly larger and glossy than the primer blend area.

    Caution

    • During operation, reserve sufficient flash-off time to ensure complete solvent evaporation.

    • Do not dry the sprayed surface with high-pressure air.

    • Uneven spraying texture may result in paint sagging or coarse textures.

  2. Finishing of spraying: Apply a wet coating so that the coating texture is uniform and consistent with the surrounding panel; otherwise, repeat spraying.

    Reminder

    • Make sure that the texture of the sprayed panel is consistent with that of the original position of the adjacent panel; otherwise, the paint mist cannot be integrated into the coating, resulting in coarse textures.

    • If necessary, perform blending after the varnish coating is applied and before the paint mist at the edge of the blend area.

    Reminder

    Texture and glossiness can be detected by a fluorescent lamp.

    • Apply several times on the wet coating.

    • After spraying, reserve sufficient flash-off time to ensure solvent evaporation.

  3. For forced drying, refer to How to dry 

    Reminder

    • If the standing and curing time is short, paint sagging or pock markings on the sprayed surface will occur.

    • Excessively high temperatures can cause deformation of plastic components.

    Reminder

    In case of small-area spraying or local repair, the paint can be baked after the paint film surface is dry and dust-free.

Scenario II: Spraying of the whole panel surface

Step I: Spray primer layer.

  1. Apply the intermediate paint. Refer to Spraying of intermediate paint 

    Caution

    This step can be ignored if the new panel is provided with intermediate paint.

  2. For preparation, refer to Preliminary preparation 

  3. Panel cleaning: After removing dust and dirt on the clearance of panels and spraying surface by using compressed air with a pressure higher than the spraying pressure, clean the panel with degreaser.

    Caution

    If the panel to be sprayed is a plastic part, remove static electricity with the electrostatic cleaning agent.

  4. Mist spraying of primer: After moving the vehicle/panel to the spray booth, spray a single coating on its surface until the glossiness of the sprayed surface reaches about 50%, and then dry the coating with a special blow dryer to ensure that the dried surface is no longer glossy.

    Caution

    • During spraying, check whether there are fish eyes on the surface. If any, increase the pressure of spraying air and spray the surface with dry spraying method to cover them.

    • After spraying, confirm glossiness and dryness with a fluorescent lamp.

    • In case of high humidity and low temperature of ambient air, carefully check the glossiness to avoid paint sagging.

    • Configuration requirements of spray gun:

      • Diameter of spray gun: 1.3 mm

      • Air pressure (gun tail): 1.5 Bar

      • Paint output: 2.5-3 turns from the fully closed position, and fully open spray pattern.

      • Spraying distance: 15 cm

      • Overlapping of spraying: 2/3

      • Spraying speed: 60 cm/s

    • Requirements for use of blow dryer:

      • Air pressure: 4 Bar

      • Distance: more than 30 cm

      • Angle: 45°

  5. Colored spraying: Apply a thin coating in several times with a spray gun, so that the substrate is completely covered and the glossiness of the sprayed surface is about 60 - 70%. Operation instructions:

    Reminder

    • After each coating is applied, dry the paint with a blow dryer until the surface glossiness disappears.

    • Repeat spraying as many times as necessary until the substrate is covered.

    • Insufficient glossiness after application will result in coarse textures.

    • Paint sagging will occur if the coating is too thick at one time.

    • Pinholes will occur if application is carried out while the previous coating has not been dried completely.

    • The spray gun should be closer to the spraying surface than the mist spraying.

    • With the increase of coatings, the absorption capacity of paint will enhance as well. Spray carefully for even glossiness of the whole surface.

    • Configuration requirements of spray gun:

      • Diameter of spray gun: 1.3 mm

      • Air pressure (gun tail): 1.5 Bar

      • Paint output: 2.5-3 turns from the fully closed position, and fully open spray pattern.

      • Spraying distance: 10 - 15 cm

      • Overlapping of spraying: 3/4

      • Spraying speed: 60 cm/s

    • Requirements for use of blow dryer:

      • Air pressure: 4 Bar

      • Distance: more than 30 cm

      • Angle: 45°

  6. Removal of spots: Apply a thin coating once or twice with a spray gun to remove coating spots and ensure that the sprayed surface is smooth with a glossiness level of about 30% - 50%.

    Reminder

    • After each coating is applied, dry the paint with a blow dryer until the surface glossiness disappears.

    • Insufficient glossiness after application will result in coarse textures.

    • Paint sagging will occur if the coating is too thick at one time.

    • Pinholes will occur if application is carried out while the previous coating has not been dried completely.

    • Configuration requirements of spray gun:

      • Diameter of spray gun: 1.3 mm

      • Air pressure (gun tail): 1.5 Bar

      • Paint output: 2.5-3 turns from the fully closed position, and fully open spray pattern.

      • Spraying distance: 15 - 20 cm

      • Overlapping of spraying: 3/4

      • Spraying speed: 60 - 80 cm/s

    • Requirements for use of blow dryer:

      • Air pressure: 4 Bar

      • Distance: more than 30 cm

      • Angle: 45°

Step II: Spray varnish coating.

  1. Mist spraying: Spray a thin layer of varnish on the panel surface. During spraying, spray the panel edge first and then the whole panel.

    Caution

    • During operation, reserve sufficient flash-off time to ensure complete solvent evaporation.

    • Do not dry the sprayed surface with high-pressure air.

    • Uneven spraying texture may result in paint sagging or coarse textures.

  2. Finishing of spraying: Spray one third of the panel each time. During spraying, spray the panel edge first. Operation instructions:

    Reminder

    • Make sure that the texture of the sprayed panel is consistent with that of the original position of the adjacent panel; otherwise, the paint mist cannot be integrated into the coating, resulting in coarse textures.

    Reminder

    Texture and glossiness can be detected by a fluorescent lamp.

    • Apply several times on the wet coating.

    • After spraying, reserve sufficient flash-off time to ensure solvent evaporation.

  3. For forced drying, refer to How to dry 

    Reminder

    • If the standing and curing time is short, paint sagging or pock markings on the sprayed surface will occur.

    • Excessively high temperatures can cause deformation of plastic components.

    Reminder

    In case of small-area spraying or local repair, the paint can be baked after the paint film surface is dry and dust-free.
