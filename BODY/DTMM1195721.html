Sheet metal protection

Reminder

  • This document mainly introduces the protective tools required in the process of sheet metal.

  • Tools in this document are general-purpose ones and can be purchased as required.

Workwear

  • Introduction: It refers to a kind of protective clothing worn in work and production, and features various protective functions, such as waterproof, dustproof and oilproof. Correct wearing can effectively prevent harmful dust, chemicals and harmful radioactive light from contacting the body, thus reducing the probability of occupational diseases.

Goggles

  • Introduction: Goggles are protective devices with transparent lenses that allow operators to clearly observe the working surface while protecting their eyes.

  • Purpose:

    • Prevent the splatters from entering the eyes during grinding.

    • Prevent solvent-based liquids from coming into contact with eyes.

    • Avoid the harm of small particles to the eyes when using an air gun.

Protective mask

  • Introduction: It is used to protect eyes and faces from the frontal damage by harmful substances such as dust, chemicals, hot gases and chips.

  • Purpose:

    • It is suitable for grinding with heavy dust and splashings, which can provide overall protection for the face.

    • Prevent the splatters from entering the eyes during grinding.

    • It can prevent metal sparks from splashing and hurting the face skin during grinding.

    • It can prevent small particles from damage to eyes when using an air gun.

Welding mask

  • Introduction: It can free the hands of welding operators, thus achieving high-quality welding and protecting the eyes and face skin of operators from radiation or scald.

  • Purpose:

    • During operation, it allows operators to clearly observe the welding area while protecting their eyes, thus improving the welding quality.

    • There is no need to repeat shielding upon welding arc striking, thus improving the working efficiency.

Dust mask

  • Purpose: It is a type of protective device used to filter dust, particles floating in the air, and some harmful gases and dust mist, and can effectively prevent toxic and harmful substances from being inhaled into the human body, thus reducing the probability of occupational diseases.

  • Instructions for use:

    • It is a disposable item. Please replace it in time if it is dirty.

    • Not all protective masks have comprehensive protective functions, and each type of mask is marked with its specific function.

    • It is necessary to select the most suitable type of mask according to the type of contamination. Please check the product manual of the mask when using the mask.

Smoke mask

Caution

It is a disposable item. Please replace it in time if it is dirty.

  • Purpose: It is a type of protective device used to filter smoke generated during welding and dust particles floating in the air, and can effectively prevent toxic and harmful substances from being inhaled into the human body, thus reducing the probability of occupational diseases.

  • Usage:

    • When wearing, wrap the bungee cord around the back of the head and neck.

    • It covers the upper part of the nose, and the upper metal strip is pinched to make the mask in close fit with the bridge of the nose.

Gas mask

  • Purpose: It is a type of protective device used to filter the poisonous gas volatilized from paint products, and dust particles floating in the air, thus reducing the probability of occupational diseases.

  • Instructions for use:

    • Tighten the adjustable bungee cord behind the head.

    • It covers the nose and mouth to be in close fit with the adjacent parts.

  • Maintenance: Regularly inspect and replace the activated carbon and the rubber seal in contact with the face.

Protective earplugs

Warning

  • Frequent exposure to loud noise may cause severe hearing impairment.

  • Do not use protective earplugs in conditions where it is necessary to know the repair situation through sound (such as welding and operation alignment rack).

  • Introduction: They are made of silicone or low-pressure foam and highly elastic polyester materials, and are in close contact with the external ears after being inserted into the acoustic meatus for sound insulation and protection of the hearing system of operators.

  • Purpose:

    • Reduce the sharp noise generated during grinding.

    • Reduce knocking noise.

    • Reduce the impact of other noises during work.

Protective earmuffs

Warning

  • Frequent exposure to loud noise may cause severe hearing impairment.

  • Do not use protective earplugs in conditions where it is necessary to know the repair situation through sound (such as welding and operation alignment rack).

Reminder

The earmuffs have a better protection function than the earplugs.

  • Introduction: They can reduce the noise that is more than the human body can bear (more than 64 dB) and protect the hearing system of operators.

  • Purpose: They can reduce the sharp noise generated during knocking and grinding.

Safety shoes

Caution

  • Technicians must wear qualified safety shoes when entering the workshop.

  • Make sure that the size is appropriate.

  • Do not leave shoe laces excessively long.

  • Purpose: They are equipped with protective steel panels in the front to prevent smashing and pressing. The bottom of the shoes is made of insulating rubber to avoid electric shock. The shoes have good anti-skid function.

Protective gloves

Warning

Do not use cotton gloves for sharp tools or objects to prevent finger injury after glove penetration.

  • Purpose: They are equipped with protective steel panels in the front to prevent smashing and pressing. The bottom of the shoes is made of insulating rubber to avoid electric shock. The shoes have good anti-skid function.

    • Protect the operator's hands when vehicle body parts are handled.

    • Protect the operator's hands when using basic operating tools.

    • Protect the operator's hands when grasping hard objects.

Solvent-resistant gloves

Warning

They are made of disposable rubber. Before use, check whether they are damaged, and replace them with new ones immediately in case of any abnormality.

  • Purpose: They can prevent the hands of operators from contacting corrosive liquids.

    • They are used to prevent contact with corrosive liquids and skin damage.

    • Protect the skin from solvent invasion during cleaning agent application.

    • Protect the skin from solvent invasion during sealant application.

    • Protect the skin from solvent invasion during contact with corrosive liquids.

Long sleeved welding gloves

  • Introduction: They are special gloves for welding, and can provide safety protection and comfort for welding operators. They are suitable for cutting, welding, handling and other purposes.

  • Purpose:

    • They are used for isolating high temperature and ultraviolet light.

    • They can be used in the working environment with strong light, welding slag and sparks.

Leather gloves

Caution

Each type of leather glove serves a specific purpose and should not be interchanged or worn casually.

  • Introduction: Leather gloves are used for operating pneumatic drill, electric drill and bench drill.

  • Purpose:

    • They can be used to grasp sharp objects to avoid scratching and pricking fingers.

    • They can provide protection during grinding or high-temperature operation.
