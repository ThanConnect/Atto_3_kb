Replacement of door sill inner panel assembly

Warning

To avoid repair accidents, the Operation specifications and requirements  shall be observed during operation.

Caution

  • Before operation, please carefully read and observe site safety and vehicle protection. For details, please refer to Site safety and vehicle protection 

  • During operation, please take safety protection measures as required. For details of safety protection, please refer to Sheet metal protection 

Reminder

  • The removal and installation methods of the left one are similar to those of the right one. Take the left one as an example.

  • The circular icon in the illustration in this document does not indicate the specific quantity of welding spots, but only indicates the approximate positions of welding spots. The specific quantity shall be subject to the actual vehicle.

  • There are various process symbols in the technical illustrations in this document. To understand their meanings, please refer to Description of diagram 

Operation tools and auxiliary materials

Reminder

The tools and auxiliary materials described in this document are recommended, which shall be subject to the actual situation.

  • Cutting plates: pneumatic saw, abrasive wheel grinder. Refer to Cutting tools 

  • Removal of welding spots: spot welding removal drill, special drill bit. Refer to Welding spot removal tool 

  • Cleaning/trimming flats: abrasive wheel grinder. Refer to Grinding tools 

  • Drilling/removal of welding spots: puncher, chisel. Refer to Auxiliary tools 

  • Cleaning: degreaser, tack cloth. Refer to Grinding tools 

  • Welding: resistance spot welder, gas shielded welder. Refer to Welding tools 

  • Anti-corrosion:

    • Tool: spray gun. Refer to Anti-corrosion tools 

    • Auxiliary materials: Refer to Anti-corrosion materials 

      • Anti-corrosion of joint surface: zinc powder conductive primer

      • Anti-corrosion of welding surface: anti-rust primer

Removal

Operating instructions:

  • To avoid repeated work, remove part of the side wall outer panel, B-pillar reinforcement plate and door sill reinforcement plate together.

  • Due to structural reasons, remove the left connecting plates of front and rear cross beams of front seat before removing the door sill inner panel.

Step I: Remove the left connecting plates of front and rear cross beams of seat

Reminder

The removal method of the left connecting plate of seat front beam is similar to that of the seat rear beam. Take the left connecting plate of seat front beam as an example.

  • Remove 1 layer of welding spots with a special drill bit, and take out the left connecting plate of seat front beam.

Step II: Remove part of the side wall outer panel.

  1. Cut the marked area with a pneumatic saw.

    Caution

    Carefully cut only 1 layer of metal parts to avoid damage to the reinforcement.

  2. Remove 1 layer of welding spots with an 8 mm special drill bit. After taking out part of the side wall outer panel, remove burrs on the cutting edge of the vehicle body panel.

Step III: Remove the door sill inner panel assembly together with other metal parts.

  1. Remove 1 layer of welding spots with an 8 mm special drill bit.

  2. Remove the welding spots after cutting the marked area with an abrasive wheel grinder.

    Caution

    To facilitate operation, cut part of the front connecting plate assembly of door sill inner panel, but do not cut it off.

    1. Cut 3 layers of metal parts.

    2. Remove the welding spots formed by continuous seam welding with an abrasive wheel grinder.

    3. Remove 2 layers of welding spots with a special drill bit.

    4. Remove 1 layer of welding spots with a special drill bit.

  3. Cut the upper part of B-pillar inner panel with an abrasive wheel grinder.

  4. After removing the welding spots, take out the door sill inner panel assembly together with other components.

    Danger

    To ensure safety, remove the underbody welding spots before this step.

    1. Remove 2 layers of welding spots with a special drill bit.

    2. Remove 1 layer of welding spots with a special drill bit.

    3. Remove the welding spots from the gap of the metal plate at an oblique angle of 45° with a chisel.

    4. Take out the door sill inner panel assembly together with other accessories.

      Caution

      This step is difficult to be performed and requires the cooperation of many people.

Installation

Caution

  • During installation of components, the number of welding spots shall be increased by about 30% on the basis of the original number.

  • During welding, the positions of original welding spots shall be avoided.

  • During welding, trial welding shall be carried out to confirm that the machine is properly adjusted. Distance between welding spots = panel thickness * 10 + 10 mm. For welding methods, refer to Resistance spot welding  and Gas shielded welding 

  • To reduce welding slag splashing, the zinc powder conductive primer at the hole position can be removed with steel wire before metal active gas welding.

  • It is recommended to use the tack cloth and degreasing cleaner to clean the joint surfaces.

Step I: Prepare the component for installation.

  1. Prepare the door sill inner panel assembly to be installed.

    1. Cut the required components and remove burrs on the cutting edge with an abrasive wheel grinder.

    2. Make holes (diameter: 8 mm) with a drill bit at the welding position of the new component.

    3. Grind the joint surface with an abrasive wheel grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

    4. Remove burrs in the circular hole.

    5. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the joint surface.

  2. Prepare the door sill reinforcement plate assembly to be installed.

    1. Mark and cut the required components by comparing with the cutting area of actual vehicle, and remove burrs on the cutting edge.

    2. Grind the joint surface with an abrasive wheel grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

    3. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the joint surface.

    4. Install the door sill composite structural reinforcement II to the door sill reinforcement plate.

  3. Prepare the B-pillar reinforcement plate assembly to be installed.

    1. Mark and cut the required components by comparing with the cutting area of actual vehicle, and remove burrs on the cutting edge.

      Caution

      • Before cutting, carefully measure and check the dimensions of required materials to ensure normal loading.

      • Keep the cut B-pillar inner panel (lower part).

    2. Make holes (diameter: 8 mm) with a special drill bit at the welding position where the new part will be installed.

    3. Grind the joint surface with an abrasive wheel grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

    4. Remove burrs in the circular hole.

    5. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the joint surface.

  4. Prepare the side wall outer panel.

    1. Mark and cut the required components by comparing with the cutting area of actual vehicle, and remove burrs on the cutting edge.

      Caution

      • Before cutting, carefully measure and check the dimensions of required materials to ensure normal loading.

      • Measure the required cutting size from the actual vehicle with components disassembled.

    2. Make holes with a diameter of 6 mm at the welding position where the new part will be installed.

      Reminder

      A puncher can be used to drill the component edge, and a drill bit can be used at a position where the puncher cannot be used.

    3. Grind the joint surface with an abrasive wheel grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

    4. Remove burrs in the circular hole.

    5. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the joint surface.

Step II: Install the door sill inner panel assembly

  1. Handle the joint surface.

    1. Remove the residue on the joint surface with a grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

    2. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the joint surface.

    3. Spray the joint surface with zinc powder conductive primer.

      Caution

      Do not spray it too thick to avoid affecting the welding quality.

  2. Fix the door sill inner panel assembly to its original position with the locking pliers, and then weld it by metal active gas welding.

  3. Use continuous seam welding.

  4. Carry out anti-corrosion treatment on the welded parts. Refer to Anti-corrosion treatment 

    1. Grind the parts that have been welded by metal active gas welding to make them smooth and flat.

    2. Remove stains on the surface of components with tack cloth and degreasing cleaner.

    3. Apply anti-rust primer to prevent corrosion on the surface of components.

Step III: Install the door sill composite structural reinforcement I.

  • Install the door sill composite structural reinforcement I on the vehicle body, and then tighten 2 fixing nuts.

Step IV: Install part of B-pillar inner panel.

  1. Handle the joint surface.

    1. Remove the residue on the joint surface with a grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

    2. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the joint surface.

    3. Spray the joint surface with zinc powder conductive primer.

      Caution

      Do not spray it too thick to avoid affecting the welding quality.

  2. Fix the part of B-pillar inner panel to its original position with the locking pliers, and then weld it by resistance welding.

Step V: Install the door sill reinforcement plate assembly.

  1. Handle the joint surface.

    1. Remove the residue on the joint surface with a grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

    2. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the joint surface.

    3. Spray the joint surface with zinc powder conductive primer.

      Caution

      Do not spray it too thick to avoid affecting the welding quality.

  2. Fix the door sill inner panel assembly to its original position with the locking pliers, and then weld it.

    1. Use resistance welding.

    2. Use continuous seam welding.

  3. Carry out anti-corrosion treatment on the welded parts. Refer to Anti-corrosion treatment 

    1. Grind the parts that have been welded by metal active gas welding to make them smooth and flat.

    2. Remove stains on the surface of components with tack cloth and degreasing cleaner.

    3. Apply anti-rust primer to prevent corrosion on the surface of components.

Step VI: Install the B-pillar reinforcement plate assembly.

  1. Fix the prepared component to the vehicle body and measure whether the data of Front door and roof  and Rear door  is correct. Refer to How to measure and align vehicle body 

  2. Handle the joint surface.

    1. Remove the residue on the joint surface with a grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

    2. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the joint surface.

    3. Spray the joint surface with zinc powder conductive primer.

      Caution

      Do not spray it too thick to avoid affecting the welding quality.

  3. Install a new B-pillar inner lower blocking piece.

  4. Fix the B-pillar reinforcement plate to its original position with the locking pliers, and then weld it.

    1. Use resistance welding.

    2. Use metal active gas welding.

  5. Weld the cutting area by continuous seam welding.

  6. Carry out anti-corrosion treatment on the welded parts. Refer to Anti-corrosion treatment 

    1. Grind the parts that have been welded by metal active gas welding to make them smooth and flat.

    2. Remove stains on the surface of components with tack cloth and degreasing cleaner.

    3. Apply anti-rust primer to prevent corrosion on the surface of components.

  7. Seal the door sill inner panel assembly.

    1. Clean the component surface with tack cloth and degreasing cleaner.

    2. Apply sealant to the finished metal joints.

  8. Re-confirm and check whether the data of Front door and roof  and Rear door  is correct.

    Reminder

    • If the data is abnormal, refer to How to measure and align vehicle body  for repairing the vehicle body

    • If the data cannot be repaired by correction, the component may need to be reinstalled.

Step VII: Install the side wall outer panel.

  1. Handle the joint surface.

    1. Remove the residue on the joint surface with a grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

    2. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the joint surface.

    3. Spray the joint surface with zinc powder conductive primer.

      Caution

      Do not spray it too thick to avoid affecting the welding quality.

  2. Install new NVH damping tape.

  3. Fix the side wall outer panel to its original position with the locking pliers, and then weld it.

    1. Use resistance welding.

    2. Use active gas shielded welding.

    3. Use continuous seam welding.

Step VIII: Install the left connecting plates of front and rear cross beams of seat.

Reminder

The installation method of the left connecting plate of seat front beam is similar to that of the seat rear beam. Take the left connecting plate of seat front beam as an example.

  1. Handle the joint surface.

    1. Remove the residue on the joint surface with a grinder until metal is exposed, and the surface is smooth and flat.

    2. Wipe off the fine dust and other sundries on the joint surface.

    3. Spray the joint surface with zinc powder conductive primer.

      Caution

      Do not spray it too thick to avoid affecting the welding quality.

  2. Fix the left connecting plate of seat front cross beam to its original position with the locking pliers, and then weld it by active gas shielded welding.

  3. Carry out anti-corrosion treatment on the welded parts. Refer to Anti-corrosion treatment 

    1. Grind the parts that have been welded by metal active gas welding to make them smooth and flat.

    2. Remove stains on the surface of components with tack cloth and degreasing cleaner.

    3. Apply anti-rust primer to prevent corrosion on the surface of components.

  4. After the vehicle body components are installed, conduct a waterproof test:

    Caution

    • Cure the sealant before the waterproof test.

    • No interior trims need to be installed before the end of waterproof test.

    • If there is a problem, find the leaking part and deal it with according to the actual situation.

    • After the waterproof test, dry and check the tested parts, and carry out anti-corrosion treatment in case of a risk of rust.
