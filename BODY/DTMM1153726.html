Spraying protection

Cleaning and degreasing process

  • Hazardous ingredient: organic solvent.

  • Hazards: breathing, skin and eyes.

  • Personal prevention and protection:

    • Reasonably wear the anti-organic gas mask, solvent-resistant gloves and protective goggles. Refer to Tools for spraying protection 

    • Operate in the well-ventilated environment and an operation room equipped with an exhaust system.

Poly-putty application process

  • Harmful ingredients: styrene and peroxide.

  • Hazards: inhalation of harmful dust or particles, skin and eyes.

  • Personal prevention and protection:

    • Reasonably wear the anti-organic gas mask, solvent-resistant gloves and protective goggles. Refer to Tools for spraying protection 

    • Operate in the well-ventilated environment and an operation room equipped with an exhaust system.

Grinding process

  • Harmful ingredients: dust and particles.

  • Hazards: inhalation of harmful dust or particles, skin and eyes.

  • Personal prevention and protection:

Paint mixing process

  • Harmful ingredients: organic solvents, isocyanate, and synthetic resin.

  • Hazards: inhalation of harmful gases, splashing to skin and eyes.

  • Personal prevention and protection:

    • Reasonably wear the anti-organic gas mask, solvent-resistant gloves and protective goggles. Refer to Tools for spraying protection

Spraying process

  • Harmful ingredients: heavy metal contained in paint mist, organic solvent, isocyanate, and synthetic resin.

  • Hazards: inhalation of harmful gases, splashing to skin and eyes.

  • Personal prevention and protection:

    • Reasonably wear the anti-organic gas mask, air supply mask, solvent-resistant gloves, protective goggles, and anti-static workwear. Refer to Tools for spraying protection 

    • Operate in an operation room equipped with an exhaust system.
