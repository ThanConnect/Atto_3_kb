Repair tool of plastic parts

Reminder

Tools in this document are general-purpose ones available on the market and can be purchased as required.

Hot air gun

  • Purpose:

    • Heat the damaged part of the plastic part for repair.

    • Assist in the removal of panels adhered by glue.

Pneumatic drill

Caution

Take care when using the pneumatic drill. Never touch the revolving bit directly by hand.

  • Purpose: It is mainly used for drilling plastic parts to prevent further expansion of fracture.

Heating lamp

Caution

Keep the heating lamp away from combustibles and explosives due to high temperature during use.

  • Purpose:

    • It is used for heating the poly-putty or primer coating on the panel surface.

    • It is used for heating repair after damage and deformation of large-area plastic panels.

Grinder

  • Introduction: There are generally three types of grinders: single-action grinder, double-action grinder and orbital grinder.

  • Purpose:

    • Single-action grinder: It is mainly used to remove the old paint film.

    • Double-action grinder: It can be used for grinding feather edges, poly-putty and paint surface.

    • Orbital grinder: It is mainly used for the grinding of poly-putty.
