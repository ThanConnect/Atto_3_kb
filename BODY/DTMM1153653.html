Quick repair of painting

Caution

  • During operation, please take safety protection measures as required. For details of safety protection, please refer to Spraying protection 

  • Before operation, please carefully read and observe site safety and vehicle protection. For details, please refer to Site safety and vehicle protection 

What is quick repair of painting?

Quick service refers to the small-area paint recovery, without sheet metal removal, installation and welding, which can be completed in 2 - 4.5 hours after simple price evaluation, for small exterior damages of the paint coating. Its characteristics are as follows:

  • Complex sheet metal and welding operations are not required.

  • Use quick-drying products with high performance together.

  • Have a standardized management process.

  • It is not necessary to remove vehicle body trims, lights and other parts.

  • Use standardized equipment and tools.

  • It takes 2-4.5 hours for quick repair.

  • Principle: For minor vehicle repairs where no panel replacement is needed, consequently without extensive removal, installation and alignment of sheet metal, it is possible to perform all the repair work in the same station. Neither vehicle movement or technician substitution is required, thus allowing for continuous and quick repair. The entire repair time can be reduced to a few hours. The quick repair of painting is developed according to this feature.

  • Operation instructions:

    • The damaged area is 30 cm2.

    • The damaged area of sheet metal after repair is within 30% of the panel.

    • It is recommended to spray varnish on the whole vehicle body panel.

    • The quick repair process is not recommended for horizontal surfaces of the vehicle body.

Reception process

  • Difference from traditional repair process of affected vehicles: reception - electromechanical part - sheet metal - painting - assembly.

  • Quick repair and claims consultant responsible for reception of vehicles requiring quick repair: insurance claims - appointment - reception - dispatching progress control - quality inspection and vehicle delivery.

  • As the vehicles requiring quick repair of painting are all with minor damage to panels, direct dispatching from reception to workshop repair can simplify the workflow, improve the efficiency and avoid a long waiting period during repair.

  • The full-time quick repair and claims consultant can control the vehicle repair progress and delivery time more effectively by using the progress board.

Staffing

  • Quick repair of painting realizes the integrated repair of sheet metal and painting. Vehicles with moderate and minor damages can be directly repaired at the quick repair station without entering the sheet metal workshop. It is very important for the claims consultant at reception to judge whether quick repair is applicable to the vehicle from a professional perspective and make reasonable overall planning and vehicle diversion.

  • The BYD authorized dealer is equipped with full-time quick repair reception and repair resources, so as to smoothly establish a green channel for quick repair of painting. Optimal allocation of vehicles requiring repair is a key factor.

Operation area, materials and equipment

  • Quick repair station: The quick repair of painting needs to combine the processes of sheet metal and painting. To shorten the repair time by highly integrating efficient operation process, equipment and tools, it is necessary to establish a special quick repair station for painting.

  • All tools, equipment and materials required in the repair process are allocated to each independent quick repair station. Full-time painting technicians are assigned to complete the whole process from poly-putty to polishing at the same station without moving the vehicle. Dry grinding process, efficient and quick-drying paint products, infrared heating lamps, etc. are also used as appropriate for repair. This reduces the waiting time during operation and improves repair efficiency.

Vehicles suitable for quick repair

  • Damage requirements: For the vehicle body panel, the exterior damage with an area equivalent to A4 paper does not require extensive sheet metal work during repair. Appropriate removal and installation, as well as some sheet metal reshaping, may be necessary. Extensive application of poly-putty is not needed.

  • Location requirements: Not all positions on the vehicle body are suitable for quick repair, and there are requirements for repair, as shown in the figure:

    • Roof and hood: They are not suitable for quick repair of painting. It is recommended that the whole panel be paint-sprayed if the horizontal area is subject to major damage.

    • Doors: Quick repair is optional for them. The color of this area is easy to repair, and attention should be paid to the color transition between panels.

    • Front bumper, door sill and rear fender: Quick repair of painting is recommended. The damaged area can be easily repaired, and the blend spraying shall be adopted for the varnish coating.

  • Color requirements:

    • Colors suitable for quick repair: Tianshan White, Ceramic White, Crystal White, Delan Black, Silver Sand Black, French Red, Hera Red, Purple Red Pearl, Athenian Silver, Rhea Silver, Mecca Gold, Streamer Brown, Dream Blue, Ice Sea Blue, Sky Blue and Vibrant Orange.

    • Colors non suitable for quick repair: Three-process White Pearl, Three-process Red Pearl, and Champagne Gold.

Description of quick repair scenarios

  • Minor damage:

    • The damage area is of A4 paper size. The damaged part is within 30%, and the damage depth does not exceed 1 cm. Scratches are generally between 5 cm and 15 cm, and the damage at lines and corners is within 7 cm.

    • Slight scratches, with the varnish layer slightly scratched.

    • No spraying is required after treatment by dry grinder.

    • The top coat can be sprayed directly.

    • One panel: The vehicle can be delivered within 3 hours.

    • Two panels: The vehicle can be delivered within 4.5 hours.

    • Three panels: The vehicle can be delivered within 6 hours.

  • Moderate damage:

    • Gravel impact marks.

    • The damage part exceeds 50%, the damage depth exceeds 3 cm, and the damage at lines and corners is more than 25 cm.

    • The scratch reaches the colored paint layer, the primer can be seen, and the bottom metal surface is not deformed.

    • No spraying is required after treatment by dry grinder.

    • Two panels: The vehicle can be delivered within 9 hours.

    • Three panels: The vehicle can be delivered within 12 hours.

  • Major damage:

    • The damage part exceeds 50%, the damage depth exceeds 3 cm, and the damage at lines and corners is more than 25 cm.

    • The scratch reaches the substrate, with slight deformation.

    • No complicated sheet metal treatment is required.

    • It can be restored to the original state by applying 1-2 layers of poly-putty thinly.

    • Two panels: The vehicle can be delivered within 9 hours.

    • Three panels: The vehicle can be delivered within 12 hours.

  • New parts for replacement:

    • Two panels: The vehicle can be delivered within 6 hours.

    • Three panels: The vehicle can be delivered within 9 hours.

Quick repair process

  1. Perform reception for sheet metal painting. Refer to Reception process 

  2. Repair the sheet metal. Refer to How to repair the outer panel 

  3. Apply poly-putty.

    1. Spray the degreaser evenly on the damaged panel with a degreaser spray pot.

    2. Wipe back and forth with a wiping cloth. Before the degreaser volatilizes, wipe it in one direction with another wiping cloth and wipe off the degreaser to clean the whole panel.

    3. Grind the feather edge.

      Caution

      • Ensure the grit jump of sandpaper not exceeding 100 (like P80-P120-P180).

      • Touch the ground feather edge with hands to check if they are smooth.

      • The width of the feather edge is 3 cm.

      • Grind with grinding head #5/7 and sandpaper #P80-120, and slightly tilt the grinding pad of grinder to the panel to shorten the grinding time.

      • Move the grinding pad along the feather edge until the width of the feather edge of each layer of the old paint film reaches about 30 mm.

      • Use a sandpaper #P180 to grind the whole part to be coated with poly-putty, and ensure that the grinding range exceeds the coating range.

    4. Clean and degrease.

      • Spray the degreaser evenly in the damaged panel area.

      • Clean with a wet wiping cloth and then dry with a dry wiping cloth.

    5. Apply poly-putty.

      1. Open the poly-putty tank to stir the poly-putty base stock evenly.

      2. Mix the poly-putty and curing agent in a ratio of 100:2-3.

      3. Apply the first thin coat of poly-putty to fill the small depressions or sandpaper marks after repair, avoiding pinholes and bubbles caused by air mixing.

      4. Apply the second coat of poly-putty which can be thicker as appropriate and pay attention to trimming to reduce poly-putty marks.

      5. Use an alternating method of lateral and longitudinal application in the subsequent process to ensure better filling.

    6. Forcibly dry it for 5-10 minutes with an infrared heating lamp.

      Reminder

      • Check if the heating lamp is located about 50 cm from the panel.

      • Check if the poly-putty is dry by touching its edge with fingernails.

    7. Grind the poly-putty.

      Caution

      • During grinding, apply carbon powder to determine the defective part of poly-putty.

      • Ensure that the grit jump of sandpaper does not exceed 100, the grinding range gradually transitions from the middle to the edge, and the sandpaper marks from previous grinding are removed.

      • Clean the repair area, check pinholes and reapply poly-putty if necessary.

      1. Apply carbon powder indicator on the poly-putty to better judge grinding quality of poly-putty.

      2. Grind the poly-putty and shape it with a suitable grinder and sandpaper #P80-120.

      3. After the poly-putty is ground flat, trim and polish it with a manual grinding panel and sandpaper #P180.

      4. Remove coarse sandpaper marks with a fine grinder and sandpaper #P240.

      5. Use grinder #3 and sandpaper #P320 to grind the surrounding paint film beyond the range of intermediate paint, and use a dust gun to remove dust in small holes on the poly-putty surface.

  4. Apply intermediate paint.

    1. Preparation before application

      1. Clean the area around the repair area. Do not wipe the poly-putty area during cleaning.

      2. Reversely mask the periphery of the part to be sprayed within the grinding range and then degrease and clean it.

    2. Spraying of intermediate paint

      Caution

      • The poly-putty surface is free of dust before spraying.

      • The first coat is applied throughout the grinding range.

      • Flash-off time is taken into account between coats.

      • The spraying range of intermediate paint is reduced layer by layer.

      1. Prepare the paint according to the mixing ratio recommended by the paint manufacturer.

      2. Spray the first thin coat of intermediate paint with a special spray gun to avoid paint lifting. The spraying range should not be too close to the masking paper. Otherwise, the paint edge may be too thick for grinding.

      3. Apply the second coat by wet spraying and keep the spraying range within the first coat as far as possible.

      4. Start applying the third coat by wet spraying after flash-off of the second coat to avoid pinholes and keep the spraying range within the second coat as far as possible.

    3. Drying of intermediate paint

      • Forcibly dry the paint surface for 20 minutes with an infrared heating lamp about 50 cm away.

      • The panel is 60 °C and dried with an infrared heating lamp for 20 minutes.

    4. Grinding of intermediate paint

      Caution

      • Every time the sandpaper is replaced, it is necessary to apply carbon powder on the edge to confirm the defective part.

      • The grinding pad is closely attached to the panel, and the radian of the panel should be taken into account.

      • During grinding, it is crucial to grind from different directions and continuously check the flatness by hand.

      1. Apply carbon powder indicator on the intermediate paint surface first.

      2. Manually grind the sprayed poly-putty area with a hand planer and sandpaper #P320 (if necessary).

      3. Use grinding head #3 and sandpaper #P400 to grind the intermediate paint area.

      4. Use sandpaper #P600 to grind the intermediate paint area.

    5. Grinding of blend area: Grind with grinding head #3 and sandpaper #P1000, or grind with gray scouring pad and abrasive paste.

      Caution

      • The grit jump of sandpaper shall be noted for grinding the transition paint surface.

      • It is recommended that the edge of the panel be treated with scouring pad and abrasive paste.

  5. Application of top coat

    1. Preparation for application

      1. Clean and degrease the panel to be sprayed. During cleaning, carefully check the defective parts of intermediate paint and repair them in time.

      2. Mask the area required to be masked with the masking paper.

      3. Use a blower gun and tack cloth to clean the dust from top to bottom.

      4. Prepare an appropriate amount of paint according to the mixing ratio recommended in the paint manual.

      5. Select a filter funnel with more than 120 meshes for filtration of primer.

    2. Spraying of plain paint

      1. Adjust the parameters of the spray gun such as the spray pattern, paint output, and pressure.

      2. Spray the first coat of colored paint on the intermediate paint surface with a gun distance of about 20 cm.

      3. After flash-off of the painted surface, spray the second coat while ensuring a proper gun distance, constant speed, overlapping range, vertical angle, parallel distance, trigger retraction and release, and wrist angle.

      4. Spray the third coat after flash-off of the second coat.

      5. Spot check

        • Spot check of 2K plain paint spraying

          • The plain paint reaches 80% gloss in the first semi-wet spraying and 100% gloss in the second full wet spraying.

          • The flash-off time of the first coat is about 15 minutes.

        • Spot check of 1K metallic paint spraying

          • For metallic paint, mist spraying, wet spraying, and semi-wet spraying are used for the first, second, and third coats, respectively.

          • The flash-off time of each coat is 5-10 minutes.

          • It is required to remove the paint mist with tack cloth before proceeding to the next coat.

          • The water-based primer should be applied properly based on the spraying method.

    3. Transitional spraying of metallic primer

      • Use transitional spraying to adjacent panels if the local repair position is close to the edge of adjacent panels.

      • Use transitional spraying to adjacent panels on both sides and then varnish all areas in case of whole panel spraying.

      • In case of single-process top coat spraying, use transitional spraying, spray the blend area with blender, and dry the area before polishing.

  6. Varnish spraying

    1. Adjust the spray gun and apply a thin coat of varnish on an area larger than the primer spraying area.

    2. Spray the second coat of varnish after flash-off to ensure fullness of the varnish while paying attention to details such as gun distance, moving speed of spray gun, vertical angle, and overlapping of spray pattern. Ensure sound coating texture and paint film brightness after spraying.

  7. Baking

    Caution

    • After the top coat is sprayed, it is required to wait for 10 minutes before heating and baking it.

    • It can be baked for 30 minutes by heating the spray booth to 60°C, or baked for 20 minutes by using an infrared heating lamp. Or let it dry naturally.

    • Forcibly dry the paint after spraying.

    • Tear off the covered adhesive tape after baking and before cooling down of the panel. Otherwise, it will be hard to tear it off after the panel cools down.

  8. Polishing

    1. After marking defects such as dust spots on the paint surface with a marker pen, grind the dust spots with a point grinder and sandpaper #P1500, or manually grind the dust spots with grinding block and waterproof sandpaper.

      Caution

      Use an appropriate grinding head and fine sandpaper #P2000 for large-area grinding.

    2. Polish with coarse wool wheel or sponge wheel and polishing wax.

    3. Polish with fine polishing wax and fine polishing wheel to restore the gloss of paint film coating.

  9. Quality inspection and vehicle delivery

    Caution

    • Make sure that the vehicle is cleaned.

    • Make sure that all repair items are completed.

    • Make all necessary preparations before delivery.

    • Make sure that the vehicle body color, panel flatness and panel gap meet the quality standards after repair.

    1. Clean the vehicle before the completion inspection. For inspection, refer to Quality spot checklist of repair process  and Inspection of repair quality 

    2. Arrange the reception claims consultant to contact the customer and make an appointment for vehicle pickup. When the customer arrives for pickup, the reception claims consultant checks the repair quality to ensure customer satisfaction.
