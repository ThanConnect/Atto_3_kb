Spraying tool

Reminder

Tools in this document are general-purpose ones available on the market and can be purchased as required.

Upper cup spray gun

  • Characteristic:

    • It is suitable for medium-sized production enterprises that require efficient large-area spraying operations.

    • Pressurize the paint with a paint pressure pot or paint pump, and deliver the pressurized paint to the nozzle of the spray gun for atomization and spraying.

  • Disadvantage: It takes more time to clean it, so it is not suitable for small-area spraying.

Upper cup spray gun (gravity feed)

  • Characteristic:

    • Generally, its capacity is about 600 ml. The vent hole on the top of the paint cup shall be kept clear.

    • The gravity feed spray gun is suitable for small-area operations, such as local repairs with low paint consumption.

    • Disposable paint cup can be used to improve efficiency and save paint.

    • It minimizes fluctuations in spraying volume due to changes in paint viscosity.

  • Disadvantage: The capacity of its paint cup is too small, making the gravity feed spray gun not suitable for large-area spraying.

Lower cup spray gun (suction feed)

  • Characteristic:

    • The capacity of its paint cup is large, making the suction feed spray gun suitable for large-area spraying.

    • Paint is sucked from the paint cup to the nozzle by vacuum suction, and then atomized and sprayed.

    • Disposable paint cup can be used to improve efficiency and save paint.

    • It minimizes fluctuations in spraying volume due to changes in paint viscosity.

  • Disadvantage: The amount of paint sprayed is significantly affected by the paint viscosity and density. Paint output becomes lower at higher viscosity, and also it is closely related to the nozzle size of the spray gun.

Top coat spray gun

  • Purpose: It is mainly used for spraying single-process topcoat, two-process paint and varnish.

  • Characteristic: Atomizing the paint into fine particles and thin paint film can ensure that the paint film texture of the sprayed top coat layer is relatively thin and uniform.

  • Instructions for use:

    • Recommended spraying distance: 15-20 cm

    • Diameter of spray gun: 1.0-1.5 mm

Primer spray gun

  • Purpose: It is mainly used for spraying anti-rust primer and intermediate paint. It is suitable for spraying operations with relatively high viscosity and thick paint film such as primer spraying.

  • Characteristic:

    • The paint is atomized evenly, but the atomization fineness is not as good as that of the top coat spray gun.

    • The spray pattern is relatively concentrated, with a large wet area in the center and a small atomization area around.

  • Instructions for use: The recommended spraying distance is 15-20 cm.

Minor repair spray gun

  • Purpose: The minor repair spray gun is designed for small-area repair, making local repair easier.

  • Characteristic: It features light weight, smaller nozzle size, easy application of thin coatings, and effective control of spraying area. When used for repair of aluminum powder paint and pearl paint, it reduces the occurrence of "black circles".

  • Instructions for use:

    • Use 0.7-2.0 bar air pressure and adjust the amount of paint output according to the shape and scope of the local area to be repaired.

    • When it is used, apply thin coating in multiple passes to slowly control color and masking.

    • Spraying distance: 10-15 cm

    • Diameter of spray gun: 0.8-1.2 mm
