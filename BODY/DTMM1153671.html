Material characteristics

Features

Unlike plain paints that only use tinting pigments and present the same color at different observation angles, metallic paints contain glittering pigments with special gloss under light and can exhibit different colors at different observation angles.

  • It produces a unique glittering effect in sunlight.

  • When viewed from the front or side, there is a great difference in the hue.

  • It exhibits excellent transparency and three-dimensional effect.

  • Difference with plain paint:

    • Different number of processes.

    • Different pigments used.

    • Different reflective effects.

Differences between wet and dry spraying processes

Due to the uniqueness of metallic paint materials, different wet and dry coatings will lead to different colors:

  • Dry coating: bright when viewed directly and dark when viewed obliquely.

  • Wet coating: dark when viewed directly and bright when viewed obliquely.
