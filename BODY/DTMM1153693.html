First aid and emergency treatment

Emergency treatment of personnel injury

The first aid trained personnel shall be assigned in the repair workshop.

The followings are some examples of immediate measures required in case of emergency:

  • If foreign matters splash into eyes, wash them carefully with clean water for at least 10 min.

  • Wash the skin contaminated by materials with soap and clean water.

  • After the body is burned, rinse it with cold water immediately to cool down and observe its condition. If it does not get better, send the injured person to hospital immediately.

  • In case of inhalation of smoke and dust, immediately go to a place with fresh air and observe the physical condition. If it does not get better, send to hospital immediately.

  • In case of swallowing of the liquid, give the information contained on the container or label to a doctor and consult. Do not attempt to vomit unless indicated otherwise on the label.

  • If other personnel get an electric shock accidentally, immediately turn off the power supply on the premise of ensuring your own safety and organize rescue.

First aid for electric shock

    1. Your own safety is the first priority. Do not touch any person who is still in contact with voltage. If conditions permit, take insulation protection measures for yourself and others, and separate the victim or conductor from the voltage with non-conductive objects (wooden boards, broom handles, etc.).

    2. Immediately turn off the power supply on the premise of ensuring safety.

    3. Carry out on-site rescue. If the victim is conscious after being separated from the power supply, leave him/her to have a quiet rest. In case of severe burns, send him/her to hospital for treatment. If the victim is in a coma but still has heartbeat and breathing, place him/her on his/her back and untie his/her clothes for smooth breathing, with good ventilation. Observe closely, and quickly ask a doctor for diagnosis and treatment or send to hospital for examination and treatment. If the victim stops breathing and heartbeating temporarily, but has not yet died, defibrillate with an automatic electronic defibrillator (AED) (if any) immediately, or perform cardiopulmonary resuscitation. The specific operation of cardiopulmonary resuscitation is as follows:

      1. Place the victim on his/her back on a wooden board or hard ground.

      2. Untie the neckline and trouser belt, and make his/her head lean back as far as possible, so that the root of tongue will not block the airway.

      3. Then open his/her mouth with hands and remove foreign matters inside to make the airway unobstructed.

      4. Hold the lower jaw with one hand and pinch the nose with the other hand. Carry out artificial respiration for about 2 s to expand the chest of the rescued person, then relax the mouth and nose to make the chest retract naturally, and exhale for about 3 s.

      5. Repeat, and blow about 12 times per minute.

      6. If the victim's mouth cannot be opened, perform mouth-to-nose artificial respiration. In this case, blow for a long time with higher pressure so that the air flows into the lungs.

      7. If the victim is a child, only blow a small amount of air into the mouth to avoid lung damage.

    4. When performing artificial respiration, apply external chest compression at a depth of about 5 cm, with about 100 compressions per minute.

    Emergency treatment for electrolyte leakage of high-voltage battery

    Judgement of high-voltage battery electrolyte:

    1. A large amount of leakage is visible to the naked eye.

    2. For a small amount of leakage, the position concerned is wet or milky white spots are formed.

    3. A pungent odor indicates a leakage.

    Emergency treatment for contact with electrolyte:

    • In case of accidental contact with leaked fluid, rinse immediately with plenty of clean water for 10 to 15 min in normal cases. If you feel pain, apply 2.5% calcium gluconate ointment, or soak in 2% to 2.5% calcium gluconate solution to relieve pain. If the pain does not relieve or symptoms of discomfort appear, seek medical attention immediately.

    • In case of contact with eyes, rinse the eyes with clean water for at least 15 min. If you still feel pain, seek medical attention immediately.

    • In case of contact with the skin, take off the contaminated clothes, wipe away the contaminants immediately with a clean cloth, and then wash thoroughly with soap and water. If you still feel pain, seek medical attention immediately.

    • In case of contact with the respiratory tract, move to the area with fresh air first. If you still have difficulty in breathing or discomfort, seek medical attention immediately. In case of apnea, perform cardiopulmonary resuscitation and seek medical attention immediately.

    Emergency definition

    Definition: An emergency refers to an abnormal fault that suddenly occurs during the normal operation of the vehicle, without predictability and corresponding preparation in advance.

    Category:

    • General faults that do not affect the personal and property safety of passengers and drivers.

    • Serious faults that affect the personal and property safety of passengers and drivers.

    Response principle:

    • Troubleshooting of general faults: The normal vehicle repair can be carried out at the service station for general faults.

    • Troubleshooting of serious faults: For serious faults, based on the principle of "life first", the life safety of corresponding personnel shall be ensured first in any emergency.
