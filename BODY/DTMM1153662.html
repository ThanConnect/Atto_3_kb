Application of sheet metal poly-putty

Caution

  • During operation, please take safety protection measures as required. For details of safety protection, please refer to Spraying protection 

  • Before operation, please carefully read and observe site safety and vehicle protection. For details, please refer to Site safety and vehicle protection 

Operation method

  1. Apply primer to the area. Refer to Primer spraying 

  2. Operating instructions for stirring poly-putty:

    Caution

    • If the poly-putty adhering to the tank is dried, particles or residues will be formed during application.

    • If the poly-putty and curing agent are not fully stirred before mixing, the poly-putty will not cure properly.

    • While rotating the poly-putty tank, move the stirring rod up and down to stir the poly-putty.

    • When stirring, do not let the poly-putty adhere to the edge of the tank. If it does, clean it with solvent.

  3. Mix the poly-putty with the curing agent.

    1. Tighten the curing agent cover and knead the entire curing agent tube to mix the curing agent.

    2. Follow the manufacturer's instructions on the mixing proportion, and place an appropriate amount of poly-putty and curing agent on a clean mixing plate respectively. Do not allow direct contact between the two.

    3. Take the curing agent with the edge of a scraper, and then draw a circle with the scraper to mix the curing agent and poly-putty to a mixing rate of 70%~80%.

    4. Shovel up about half of the poly-putty, scrape it onto the remaining poly-putty, press the poly-putty to spread it evenly, and stir until the color is uniform.

      Caution

      • When starting to mix poly-putty and curing agent, mix the components within 1 minute, as the poly-putty will start to cure and become too hard to apply after 5 minutes.

      • Inadequate mixing of ingredients can result in poor curing.

    5. Squeeze and scrape poly-putty thin to remove any air, so as to prevent pinholes and improve the adhesion of poly-putty.

  4. Thin application of poly-putty: Apply a thin layer of poly-putty to the area where epoxy primer has been applied, and fill in minor scratches or depressions caused by welding. Operation instructions:

    • Ensure that poly-putty is applied to the grinding area.

    • Hold the scraper with palm facing down, gently support the scraper with index and middle fingers in a V-shape, and shovel a small amount of poly-putty onto the edge of the scraper.

    • When holding the scraper, only make the edge of the scraper touch the surface of the panel, and then press firmly to apply a thin layer of poly-putty.

  5. Application of poly-putty: Scrape each layer of poly-putty thinly and apply it in multiple times. The application height shall be higher than the undamaged reference surface. Operation instructions:

    Caution

    • If a large amount of poly-putty is applied in one time, air will be trapped in the poly-putty, resulting in pinholes.

    • If the poly-putty cannot be applied in one time, wait for the edge of the applied poly-putty to dry to a touchable degree, and then proceed to the application of next layer of the poly-putty.

    • Hold the scraper with palm facing down and gently support the scraper with index and middle fingers in a V-shape to apply poly-putty, and overlap the previous layer of poly-putty by 1/3 each time. Erect the scraper to apply a thin layer, and lower the scraper to apply a thick layer.

    • Press the scraper against the steel panel, scrape the surrounding poly-putty layer thin, and move the scraper along the shape of the feather edge.

  6. Trimming of poly-putty: Trim all steps around the edge of poly-putty and remove all poly-putty spreading beyond the grinding area. Operation instructions:

    • Ensure that there are no steps between the poly-putty application area and the old coating.

    • Ensure that there is no residual poly-putty on the surface beyond the grinding area.

    • Press the scraper against the coating side and move it along the shape of the feather edge. The coating of the edge shall be scraped into a thin layer.

  7. Dry poly-putty: Forcibly dry poly-putty to ensure that it can be ground. Operation instructions:

    • After the poly-putty is dried to a touchable degree, forcibly dry it with a heating lamp. Avoid excessively high lamp temperature during drying.

      Caution

      If the poly-putty is heated rapidly during drying, it may crack or peel off.

    • Gently place sandpaper on the thinner part of the coating, and observe whether poly-putty becomes powder during grinding to determine whether the poly-putty is dry.

    • Use fingernail to gently scratch the thinner edge of poly-putty. If white marks appear on the surface, it indicates that the poly-putty is completely dry.

    • The use of forced drying of the poly-putty will shorten the curing time of the poly-putty and improve working efficiency.

  8. Rough grinding of poly-putty: Grind the rough poly-putty area with sandpaper #80 or #120 to form a relatively smooth surface. Ensure that the poly-putty surface is free from steps, large dents, and bulges. Operation instructions:

    Caution

    • In order to avoid deep scratches on the steel panel, make sure that the thickness of grinding disc is appropriate before switching on the grinder.

    • The steel panel that is still hot after drying with a heating lamp will expand, and the radian of the entire steel panel will be different. Before grinding, it is necessary to confirm that the panel temperature is normal.

    • Before grinding, it is necessary to confirm that poly-putty is completely dry.

    • Apply a carbon powder guide layer to the poly-putty application area, which is helpful in determining the grinding range and flatness.

    • Stand at a position where the grinding plate can easily reach the entire area of poly-putty for grinding.

    • Select a suitable grinding head and paste sandpaper, hold the grinding head from below, gently press it against the poly-putty surface, and grind to a distance of 10 mm within the edge of poly-putty. Grind only the poly-putty surface, and do not leave sandpaper marks on undamaged areas.

    • After grinding the higher part, grind the entire surface, taking care to avoid local dents in the poly-putty area.

    • Check whether the dents and bulges are ground evenly through visual inspection and hand touch.

  9. Fine grinding of poly-putty: Apply carbon powder to the poly-putty surface, and then grind the poly-putty surface to 80% surface flatness with a grinding block or grinder equipped with sandpaper #180. Provide a natural and smooth transition between the old coating and the surface applied with poly-putty. Operation instructions:

    • Ensure that the entire feather edge is faintly visible at the edge of poly-putty.

    • Stand in a suitable position to grind the poly-putty surface only, and do not leave sandpaper marks on the undamaged surface.

      Caution

      • The grinding shall be carried out in a multi-angle or multi-directional star-shaped manner to ensure the smooth surface of the poly-putty after grinding.

      • During the grinding, the flatness of the entire surface shall be checked visually and by hand.

      • During the grinding, both manual and machine methods can be used.

    • Method for checking for dents and protrusions:

      • Touch the surface with hands.

      • If necessary, check the surface flatness with a ruler.

      • Check for small dents and bulges with a grinding indicator.

  10. Fine grinding of poly-putty: Grind the entire poly-putty surface until the surface smoothness reaches 90% with a manual grinding plate equipped with sandpaper #240, providing a natural and smooth transition between the old coating and the surface applied with poly-putty. Operation instructions:

    • Grind the entire poly-putty application range and 5 cm beyond the grinding area to ensure a smooth surface.

    • Check the surface for dents or bulges during grinding, and check the transition between the poly-putty surface and the undamaged surface by hand touch or a ruler.

    • Check for coarse sandpaper marks after grinding with a grinding indicator.

    • After grinding, blow compressed air onto the poly-putty surface to check for pinholes. If there are pinholes on poly-putty, apply a thin layer of poly-putty with the same method for thin application and perform re-grinding.

  11. Final grinding of poly-putty: Grind with a grinding head/plate equipped with sandpaper #320 to remove all sandpaper marks left during surface trimming. Operation instructions:

    Caution

    In order to prevent deep scratches on steel panel, the grinder shall be used to grind areas other than poly-putty before grinding to make corresponding areas tarnished; otherwise, the coating may fall off.

    • Adjust the double-action grinder to the appropriate speed to achieve the best grinding operation.

    • Grind the surface exceeding the poly-putty edge by about 10 cm, ensuring that the area to be applied with primer has been ground and the surface is no longer glossy. Be careful not to go too far beyond the area that needs to be repainted.

  12. Repair of defects.

    • Repair excessively high areas:

      • Defect description: The steel panel is higher than the undamaged surface and exposed.

      • Solution: Use tools such as a pointed hammer to repair the higher parts, so that they are lower than the undamaged surface. Remove the poly-putty around the repaired part with sandpaper #120 and then trim the part.

    • Repair lower areas:

      Caution

      Applying the poly-putty only in the lower part makes it difficult to achieve the natural transition of the poly-putty to the surrounding parts.

      • Defect description: a lower area formed due to excessive grinding.

      • Solution: After cleaning and degreasing, apply a thin layer of poly-putty to the entire surface. After applying the remaining poly-putty to the lower area, apply poly-putty to the entire surface.

    • Repair pinholes:

      • Defect description: There are pinholes on the surface that cannot be filled with intermediate paint or nitrocellulose poly-putty.

      • Solution: After cleaning and degreasing, apply a thin layer of poly-putty to the entire surface, and fill only the pinholes with the same method for thin application. Then dry the surface with a heating lamp.
