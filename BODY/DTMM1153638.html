Introduction to sheet shrinking process

Caution

  • During operation, please take safety protection measures as required. For details of safety protection, please refer to Sheet metal protection 

  • Before operation, please carefully read and observe site safety and vehicle protection. For details, please refer to Site safety and vehicle protection 

The extended panel fails to provide sufficient rigidity, and the extended bulge also makes it impossible to apply the poly-putty. Therefore, it is necessary to improve the strength of steel panels through shrinking process and make the excessive bulge contract. The method is to heat the steel panel and then rapidly cool it down, so as to recover the original shape of the extended area of the steel panel by virtue of heat expansion and cold contraction.

Operation process

  1. Preparation for sheet metal shrinking

    • Grind the paint film or other impurities on the surface of the panel.

    • Select an appropriate position to connect the GND wire.

    • Carry out a trial operation on waste panels of the same material and thickness.

  2. Suitable fire shrinking method shall be adopted for fire shrinking as required:

    Carbon rod shrinking: Carbon rod shrinking is applied to the large-area extended repaired part of the vehicle body's outer panel. According to the size of the raised position, a heated carbon rod can be used to draw a spiral or circle shape. The circle drawing should be conducted from large to small and from outside to inside. The operation steps are as follows:

    Caution

    • Do not use oxyacetylene shrinking, which will reduce the strength and durability of the panels.

    • Only air guns can be used for cooling during shrinking, and water cooling is prohibited.

    1. Adjust the shrinking mode of the welding machine.

    2. After the carbon rod contacts the panel and is heated, use a sheet metal hammer for knocking.

    3. Use an air gun for cooling.

    4. Cool for 5-6s.

    Copper bar shrinking: The copper bar can only be used for point shrinking, and the operation steps are as follows:

    Caution

    During shrinking, ensure that the electrode tip and panel are clean and free of other stains. Otherwise, the panel may be burned through.

    1. Adjust the shrinking mode of the welding machine.

    2. Use a copper rod to come into contact with the panel and apply force to press the panel in the extended position.

    3. Heat for 1-2s.

    4. Quickly cool the heated area with an air gun.

FAQs

Dented or perforated panels (undesirable case)

  • Phenomenon: Noticeable dents or perforations are generated on the surface of the outer plate after the shrinking operation.

  • Cause:

    • Inappropriate adjustment of welding machine parameters and excessive current

    • Dirt or impurities on the surface of the panels

    • Too long power-on time

  • Solution:

    • Adjust the equipment parameters to shorten the power-on time.

    • Clean impurities or residual paint film on the shrinking surface.

    • In case of any hole, fill it with a welding machine before handing over the corresponding panel to the next process.
