How to measure and align vehicle body

Caution

  • During operation, please take safety protection measures as required. For details of safety protection, please refer to Sheet metal protection 

  • Before operation, please carefully read and observe site safety and vehicle protection. For details, please refer to Site safety and vehicle protection 

Vehicle body measurement

Select measuring tools according to actual needs: measuring tape, ruler, universal measuring ruler, and electronic measuring platform. Refer to Vehicle body measurement 

Reminder

The following data can help you measure the vehicle body of this model:

  • Front compartment 

  • Front door and roof 
  • Rear door 
  • Rear compartment 
  • Underbody 

Before vehicle body alignment, it is necessary to measure the vehicle body first to evaluate the correction scheme. The methods of measuring the vehicle body are as follows:

  • Comparison method: First measure the dimension of the undamaged side and then the dimension of the damaged side, and compare the two dimensions to determine the drawing extent for repair.

  • Bilateral symmetry method: Measure the two diagonals of the vehicle body frame structure and compare whether they are the same, so as to judge whether the vehicle body is deformed.

  • Vehicle body centerline method: Hang an appropriate number of vehicle body rulers at the correct position on the underbody, and judge whether the vehicle body is deformed by observing whether the center points of all vehicle body rulers are in the same straight line.

Vehicle body alignment

Select the correct alignment equipment and method before vehicle body alignment:

  • Alignment equipment: lift-type alignment rack, removable alignment rack, small damage repair and alignment rack, platform-type alignment rack, ground-cage alignment rack, pull tower, fixture, vehicle body fixing bracket, drawing fixture, edge-clamped drawing fixture, flat drawing fixture, hook-type drawing fixture, two-way drawing fixture, and safety cable (This part needs to match the link of the corresponding tool)

  • Alignment methods and skills:

    • Vector drawing:

      • In terms of the direction and angle of multiple forces to be combined or divided, the vector can be divided into resultant force and component force.

      • As shown in the figure, the effect of drawing F1 and F2 simultaneously is the same as that of drawing F3 alone.

      • In some special positions, the vector drawing of force decomposition principle must be adopted.

    • Multi-point drawing:

      • Reverse single-point drawing may be used for slight deformation.

      • Serious or large-scale deformation of the vehicle body in a collision can be corrected by multi-point reverse drawing to avoid rupture and uneven tension.

      • In case of multi-point drawing, the fixtures do not need to clamp in one position. Multiple points with severe deformation can be selected to secure the fixtures, and multiple pull towers or steel chains can be used together.

      Note: The angle between the chain and the hydraulic cylinder is 90° generally, exceeding which uneven stress will occur, resulting in damage to the chain.

    • No auxiliary support for drawing: Before drawing alignment, it is necessary to confirm whether auxiliary support is required. Otherwise, secondary damage will be caused.

    • Use of auxiliary support for drawing: Proper use of auxiliary support will not cause secondary damage, but improves the drawing efficiency.

    • No auxiliary fixation for drawing: Before drawing alignment, it is necessary to confirm whether auxiliary fixation is required. Otherwise, secondary damage will be caused.

    • Use of auxiliary fixation for drawing: Proper use of auxiliary fixing support will not cause secondary damage, but improves the drawing efficiency.

    • Drawing technique of front longitudinal beam: Multi-point drawing can be adopted to improve efficiency.

    • Sill panel drawing technique: If the sill panel is subject to large-area deformation and damage, it can be drawn by inserting a round stick.

    • Rear fender drawing technique: In order to improve the drawing efficiency, before removal, the rear fender with large-area damage can be drawn by welding auxiliary drawing points.

Alignment steps

  1. Check the vehicle body data and measure the vehicle body damage with a measuring tool. Refer to Vehicle body measurement 

  2. Select the appropriate equipment as required and install it correctly.

  3. Confirm whether the vehicle body fixture is fastened.

  4. Select the appropriate drawing point, and install the drawing tools.

  5. Install the safety cable.

    Danger

    • Before vehicle alignment, install the safety cable to prevent personal injury or other damages due to breakage and falling of drawing tools.

    • Connect one end of the safety cable to the steel chain close to the fixture, and the other end to the vehicle body position to be aligned.

    • Do not knock the steel chain during drawing to prevent breakage.

  6. Install the air source joint.

    Reminder

    Before drawing alignment, ensure that the air source pressure is sufficient and there is no air leakage at the joint.

  7. Perform alignment, with precautions as follows:

    • During alignment, for sake of the safety, the operators must stand on the side of the drawing direction during operation and pay attention to the safety of surrounding vehicles and personnel.

    • Pay attention to keeping the chain at a drawing angle of 90° with the drawn part during alignment.

    • Do not align excessively. Measure and draw many times to reach the final state.

    • During alignment, pay attention to the change of gaps between doors, between the door and front fender and other components.

  8. Measurement after alignment:

    • During or after drawing, it is necessary to confirm the extent of this drawing by measuring the size so as to be aware of the extent required for the next drawing.

    • Measure the total change, judge the drawing effect, and prevent secondary damage to the vehicle caused by excessive stretching.

  9. Knock the corresponding part of the panel to remove the stress after alignment.

  10. After secondary alignment according to the above steps, check the change of relevant data and measure whether the value at each point reaches the ideal value.

    • Error: ≤ ±2 mm
