Anti-corrosion treatment

Caution

  • During operation, please take safety protection measures as required. For details of safety protection, please refer to Sheet metal protection 

  • Before operation, please carefully read and observe site safety and vehicle protection. For details, please refer to Site safety and vehicle protection 

There are roughly three anti-corrosion methods, each corresponding to different parts:

  • Front beam joints, chassis, hubcaps, floor joints and spare tire grooves: applied with spray-applied anti-corrosion materials.

  • Edge wrapping of new vehicle body panels, doors, hoods, rain gutters on the roof, welds, weld edges and joints of replaced panels : applied with extrusion-type anti-corrosion materials.

  • Joints between the interior of the trunk and the panel of the front compartment: applied with brush-applied anti-corrosion materials.

Sealing and anti-corrosion of front beam joints, chassis, hubcaps, floor joints and spare tire grooves

Danger

The material is flammable and explosive. Please confirm that the work area is free of factors leading to such risk before operation.

  • Spray-applied anti-corrosion materials are generally used for front beam joints, chassis, hubcaps, floor joints and spare tire grooves, with operations as follows:

    1. Connect the high-pressure air required by the pneumatic glue gun.

    2. Adjust the required spraying pressure according to the construction requirements.

    3. Clean the spraying area so that it is free of impurities such as water stains, oil stains and dust.

    4. Put the sprayable sheet metal sealant into the glue gun, spray anti-corrosion sealant in the corresponding area, and keep the gun nozzle 10-20 cm away from the spraying surface.

    Note: Sprayable chassis materials shall be used for spraying the chassis.

    Caution

    Chassis sealant can only be used on the underbody.

Anti-corrosion of inaccessible locations

  • The cavity anti-corrosion wax can be used for anti-corrosion of inaccessible locations. The spray gun with a hose has a large spraying angle, which can be changed to cover the surface of the inner cavity in almost all positions.

Edge wrapping of new vehicle body panels, doors, hoods, rain gutters on the roof, welds, weld edges and joints of replaced panels

Caution

  • The sealing performance of the outer panel is particularly important to ensure that water does not flow into the vehicle and avoid problems such as rusting.

  • After sealant application, trim the sealant with a hard brush to make its appearance consistent with that of the original vehicle.

  • Anti-rust sealant on the panel surface will make it difficult for the paint to adhere to the panel surface, so it is necessary to thoroughly clean up the residual anti-rust sealant.

The extrusion-type anti-corrosion materials are generally used for those positions, with operations as follows:

  1. Clean the spraying area so that it is free of impurities such as water stains, oil stains and dust.

  2. Put the extrusion-type sealant into a glue gun and apply anti-corrosion sealant to corresponding area.

Joints between the interior of the trunk and the panel of the front compartment

Caution

During operation, clean the spraying area so that it is free of impurities such as water stains, oil stains and dust.

The brush-applied anti-corrosion materials are generally used for those positions, including brush-applied anti-rust primer and brush-applied sealant, with operations as follows:

  • Brush-applied anti-rust primer: After welding, brush the welding slag and burnt paint film with a steel brush, blow it clean with an air gun, and then apply a certified primer on exposed steel panels.

  • Brush-applied sealant: Some parts that are easy to be wet for a long time and those with more water flow call for special treatment.

  • Conductive primer: Before resistance spot welding, please apply conductive primer to the joint.
