Color mixing of plain paint

Caution

  • During operation, please take safety protection measures as required. For details of safety protection, please refer to Spraying protection 

  • Before operation, please carefully read and observe site safety and vehicle protection. For details, please refer to Site safety and vehicle protection 

  • In order to ensure the quality of color mixing, it is necessary to understand Basic principles  of color mixing before operation.

Obtaining formula

In the actual color mixing, some vehicles have color codes and color formulas, while some vehicles do not have color-related information. The color information shall be reasonably distinguished according to the following color mixing process.

  • With color data: It is extremely important to confirm the information on the color being mixed by consulting the color data provided by the paint manufacturer. Fully understand and get familiar with the relevant color information so as to shorten the time for color mixing, and avoid problems that may arise during the color mixing process.

    • Color mixing process: Confirming the vehicle body color (color code) -> obtaining formula data -> checking color swatch and vehicle body color -> color mixing by measurement -> color fine-tuning after trial spraying and color comparison.

    • Obtaining formula: After logging in LMS homepage , click Data center -> Technical data -> Sheet metal painting data -> OEM certified paint repair scheme in turn and select the brand of paint used for the corresponding vehicle. Note: For information on the brand, refer to Paint color code of this vehicle model 

  • Without color data: In this case, the color mixing is different only in determining the same color swatch and preliminary color mixing process.

    Caution

    In case of uncertainty about the accuracy of the color data, it is recommended to mix a small amount of paint to confirm the color.

    Reminder

    During mixing, the dosage of corresponding paint shall be recorded to facilitate sorting and filing of color formulas.

    • Color mixing process: Looking for similar color swatches to compare with the vehicle body color -> obtaining formula data -> confirming the same color -> selecting the primary color formula -> color mixing by measurement -> color fine-tuning after trial spraying and color comparison.

    • Obtaining formula: Look for similar color swatches to compare with the vehicle body color, and fine-tune the paint color after obtaining the formula.

Step 1: color mixing by measurement

  1. Formula determination: Determine the color formula according to the vehicle body color number and vehicle model color swatch.

    Caution

    • If one color number corresponds to multiple formulas, select the color that is close to the color of the target vehicle.

    • Select the appropriate environment for color comparison.

  2. Color masterbatch preparation: The color masterbatch for color mixing must be mixed for at least 15 minutes every morning and evening with the paint mixer. If a new color masterbatch is used, it can be vibrated with an oscillator for 20 minutes before opening the barrel or stirred evenly with a paint mixing ruler before installing the slurry cover.

    Reminder

    Clean the slurry cover and openings after each use. Please store the new tank of color masterbatch upside down to avoid pigment precipitation.

  3. Color mixing by measurement: Follow the corresponding color formula, pour the corresponding color masterbatch into the paint mixing cup on the electronic scale, and ensure that the paint is accurately weighed and mixed evenly. Operation instructions:

    • Use a plastic container or a metal container with internal coating for the water-based color masterbatch paint. Do not use metal containers directly.

    • Select a container of appropriate size according to the required amount of paint and thinner.

    • Accurately weigh the color masterbatch paint and add the color masterbatch in the formula one by one.

    • Tilt the color masterbatch barrel and then gradually pull the lever of stirring cover to slowly pour out the paint.

    • Repeatedly stir the paint and scrape off all paint adhering to the inner wall of the container with a paint mixing ruler.

    Note: The following figure shows two states of the paint that has been stirred.

    • Left: not stirred evenly

    • Right side: stirred evenly

Step 2: color fine-tuning after trial spraying and color comparison

In daily operations, you can choose method I  or method II  to fine-tune colors according to your needs.

Method I: Scrape the paint to the color test plate and then fine-tune the color.

It refers to the process in which the paint color is continuously close to the target color by color comparison and adding missing colors after the color is applied on the color test plate with a paint mixing ruler or stirring rod.

  1. Painting color test plate: Apply the paint on the color test plate into a triangle with a paint mixing ruler until the background color of the test plate is covered, and then dry the plate forcibly. Operation instructions:

    • Apply the paint to a range of about 30 mm from the edge of the color test plate.

    • Do not add the thinner, which will reduce the covering power of the paint.

    • Apply thinly in multiple times.

    • Use a primed color plate for water-based color masterbatch paint. Do not apply directly to a bare metal color test plate.

  2. Color comparison: Select the appropriate light source, compare the color of the dried painted color plate with the color of accessories at the damaged parts of the vehicle, and identify the difference between the color plate and the vehicle body color according to the color, hue, lightness, and saturation .

    Reminder

    • If you stare at a color for a long time, your eyes will get used to the color, so it is difficult to judge the color difference. Therefore, it is recommended to take intermittent breaks when comparing colors to allow your eyes to regain judgment ability.

    • Conduct color comparisons under at least 2 different light sources before finalizing the color comparison to prevent metamer.

    • The high-intensity light is required for comparisons of dark colors, while the light for comparisons of light colors is not satisfying for comparisons of dark colors.

  3. Color fine-tuning: After adding the missing color masterbatch at 10% each time, repeat the above steps and paint the color test plate until the color is close to the target color. Confirm the required color masterbatch. Refer to How to identify the missing color 

    Caution

    • When adding colors, do not mix two colors simultaneously, as it may be difficult to determine which color is causing issues when problems arise.

    • The high-intensity light is required for comparisons of dark colors, while the light for comparisons of light colors is not satisfying for comparisons of dark colors.

Method II: Spray the paint on the color test plate and then fine-tune the color.

It refers to the process in which the paint color is continuously close to the target color by color J comparison and adding missing colors after the color is applied on the test plate with a spray gun.

  1. Paint mixing: According to the required proportion, pour the curing agent, thinner, and other paints into the container and mix them. Operation instructions:

    Caution

    • Add water-based thinner to the water-based paint.

    • Select the appropriate curing agent and thinner according to the temperature of the baking barn.

  2. Spraying color test plate: Spray the paint on the color test plate. Operation instructions:

    Reminder

    When spraying the first color plate, use the test paper for covering power to confirm the color covering, so as to avoid poor covering power.

    1. Filter the thinned paint with a funnel and pour it into the spray gun.

    2. Set the same spraying conditions as those for spraying vehicles, then apply several layers of paint to its surface, and reserve appropriate flash-off time.

    3. Apply the varnish coating until the coating surface becomes smooth.

  3. Drying color test plate: Ventilate the sprayed panel and allow it to stand still in the spray booth for 10-20 minutes, and dry it at 60°C for about 30 minutes.

    Caution

    • If the standing and curing time is short, paint sagging or pock markings on the sprayed surface will occur.

    • Excessively high temperatures can cause deformation of plastic components.

  4. Color comparison: Select the appropriate light source, compare the color of the dried painted color plate with the color of accessories at the damaged parts of the vehicle, and identify the difference between the color plate and the vehicle body color according to the color, hue, lightness, and saturation .

    Caution

    • If you stare at a color for a long time, your eyes will get used to the color, so it is difficult to judge the color difference. Therefore, it is recommended to take intermittent breaks when comparing colors to allow your eyes to regain judgment ability.

    • Conduct color comparisons under at least 2 different light sources before finalizing the color comparison to prevent metamer.

    • The high-intensity light is required for comparisons of dark colors, while the light for comparisons of light colors is not satisfying for comparisons of dark colors.

  5. Color fine-tuning: After adding the missing color masterbatch at 10% each time, repeat the above steps and paint the color test plate until the color is close to the target color. Confirm the required color masterbatch. Refer to How to identify the missing color 

    Caution

    • When adding colors, do not mix two colors simultaneously, as it may be difficult to determine which color is causing issues when problems arise.

    • The high-intensity light is required for comparisons of dark colors, while the light for comparisons of light colors is not satisfying for comparisons of dark colors.
