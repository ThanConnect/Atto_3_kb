Intermediate paint

Reminder

This document introduces the auxiliary materials involved in application of intermediate paint. The specific brands and models of auxiliary materials shall be subject to the actual situation.

Type I: polyurethane intermediate paint

  • Characteristic: Reliable operation performance, good filling property and easy polishing.

  • Operation instructions:

    • For intermediate primers of some paint brands, the color or the grayscale value of the primer can be adjusted to facilitate better coverage of colored paint and reduce paint consumption.

    • The primer shall be mixed with a curing agent and thoroughly stirred before use. After the primer is applied, it needs to be dried with an infrared heating lamp to speed up the drying process.

    • It shall be applied to a suitable film thickness to fill the sand holes or sandpaper marks on the surface of the poly-putty. 2-3 layers of high-performance intermediate paint shall be applied, and the film thickness can reach 120-150 μm.

    • During application, appropriate solvent evaporation time shall be reserved for application of coatings to avoid pinholes or pock markings.

  • Operation steps: Refer to Spraying of intermediate paint 

Type II: nitrocellulose intermediate paint

  • Characteristic:

    • It dries quickly and is easy to be ground, with good construction operability. However, compared with the polyurethane intermediate paint, it has slightly poor adhesion, coating thickness, filling property and isolation performance.

    • It is suitable for repair of small panel damage. For large panel damage, it is recommended to select two-component intermediate paint to ensure the repair quality.

  • Ingredient: one-component product, mainly composed of nitrocellulose resin.

  • Operation instructions:

    • It shall be applied to a suitable film thickness to fill the sand holes or sandpaper marks on the surface of the poly-putty. 2-3 layers of high-performance intermediate paint shall be applied, and the film thickness can reach 120-150 μm.

    • During application, appropriate solvent evaporation time shall be reserved for application of coatings to avoid pinholes or pock markings.

  • Operation steps: Refer to Spraying of intermediate paint
