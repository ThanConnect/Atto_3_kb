Sheet metal protection

Caution

Personal protection is the last barrier to protect the safety of operators and prevent them from injury. Proper use of protection articles, as an auxiliary preventive measure, can prevent or reduce the occurrence of industrial accidents and prevent hazards such as occupational diseases and poisoning.

Occupational disease prevention

In the process of vehicle repair, repair personnel need to use various tools and equipment, contact with various toxic and harmful substances, and there are various potential risks. If protective measures are not taken, industrial accidents and occupational diseases may be caused. This content introduces the factors of occupational diseases and preventive measures in sheet metal spraying operation.

Causes of occupational diseases:

  • Inhalation of harmful dust particles without safety protection.

  • Long-term stay in radioactive environment without safety protection.

  • Exposure to hazardous chemicals without safety protection.

Preventive measures:

  • To get an in-depth understanding of the scenarios and substances that may be harmful to human during operations, refer to Safety instructions 

  • Use protective equipment in accordance with the operating specifications. Please refer to Sheet metal protection tools  for details

Welding protection

This part describes the possible hazards to operators during welding and the corresponding avoidance methods.

Hazards during welding:

  • Strong light and radiation hazards

  • Smoke and poisonous gas

  • High temperature and splashing

Body parts damaged:

  • Ultraviolet radiation to eyes

  • Damage to respiratory system by smoke

  • Scalds and burns to skin by splashing

Avoidance method:

Wear the following protective equipment reasonably:

  • Workwear

  • Welding mask

  • Long sleeved welding gloves

  • Smoke mask

  • Safety shoes

Manual shaping protection

This section describes the hazards that may be caused to operators during manual shaping and the corresponding avoidance methods.

Hazards during manual shaping:

  • A cut on the hand.

  • Sharp noise.

  • Foreign matters enter the eyes.

Body parts damaged:

  • Damage to the operator's skin

  • Damage to the operator's hearing

  • Injury to the operator's eyes

Avoidance method:

Wear the following protective equipment reasonably:

  • Workwear

  • Safety shoes

  • Goggles

  • Protective earplugs

  • Cotton gloves
