Factors affecting welding

  • Effect of welding current:

    • The current is too small: The heat will be too low to completely melt the joint position of the welded panel, resulting in insufficient welding strength.

    • The current is too large: The heat will be too high, which will deform the welding position of the welded panel. At the same time, the heat-affected area will be too large, and the strength will be reduced.

    Undesirable case:

  • Effect of welding time:

    Reminder

    Reasonable welding time will ensure moderate heat, no obvious deformation at welding spots and good welding quality.

    • Excessive time: It will result in an excessively large heat-affected range around the welded area, but will not increase the weld strength. The heat will be too large, resulting in excessive weldment fusion or cracks at welding spots.

    • Too short time: The heat will be insufficient, resulting in no nugget or a too small welding spot, and the fusion and strength of welding spots will be insufficient.

    Undesirable case:

  • Effect of welding pressure:

    • Too low pressure: The fusion welding will not be tight enough, resulting in strength reduction.

    • Too high pressure: The reduction of resistance will lead to a decrease in welding heat, resulting in smaller base metal welding spots and lower welding strength.

    Undesirable case:

  • Effect of welding distance:

    Caution

    • The spot spacing formula is S = 10 t + 10 mm (t = plate thickness).

    • The number of welding spots at the resistance spot welding position of the replaced panel is about 1.3 times that of the OEM.

    • Too long distance: The overall connection strength of panels will be insufficient due to too few connecting points.

    • Appropriate distance: The overall connection strength increases accordingly.

    • Too short distance: Current will flow through other completed welding spots (i.e., the diversion effect). This will cause insufficient strength of the welding spots completed later.

  • Influence of welding spot edge distance:

    Caution

    Mark the welding spots before welding to determine their precise locations and avoid the welding positions being too close to the edges.

    • Too close to the edge: The welding spot will easily crack.

    • Correct margin: About 6 mm from the center of the welding spot to the edge of steel panel.

      Undesirable case:

Operation process

Caution

  • If the welder has sufficient power, three-layer steel panel welding can be carried out directly on the vehicle body.

  • When the power of the welder cannot meet the three-layer welding requirements, two layers of steel panels shall be welded first, and the third layer of steel panel shall be welded at the welding spots of the two layers of steel panels.

  • Failure to follow the above principles will result in the degradation of welding quality.

  1. Grinding panel: Use a grinder to remove the old paint film, grind and clean up the old paint film in the welding area to ensure the conductive effect.

    Caution

    Using a grinding tool with too large particles to grind the panel will reduce the strength of the panel.

  2. Clean and degrease.

    Caution

    Wear protective equipment properly during cleaning, such as masks and solvent-proof gloves.

    • Clean the welding surface with a wiping cloth dampened with detergent.

    • A clean, non-contaminated surface allows current to pass smoothly through the steel panel.

  3. Spray conductive primer: Spray special primer for spot welding on the steel panel welding contact surface.

  4. Positioning panel: After positioning the steel panel to be replaced, fix it with a locking clamp.

    Caution

    • To avoid the diversion effect, the locking clamp shall not be too close to the welding spot to be welded.

    • Ensure that there is no gap between the contact surfaces of welded panels.

  5. Welder commissioning

    • Set the welding parameters, including application pressure, welding current, and power-on time.

    • Carry out a destructive test on the welding spots after the trial welding.

  6. Welding

    Caution

    • Ensure that the panel joint surfaces and other surfaces are clean.

    • Ensure that the two electrode tips are vertically aligned.

    • The electrode must be perpendicular to the surface of the steel panel.

    • Keep the distance between the two electrodes, the distance from the welding spots to the edges, and the cooling time appropriate.

Inspection method of welding quality

  • Non-destructive test methods:

    Reminder

    After the two sample plates are separated, if a round hole with proper diameter is left on one side of the steel panel and a bump remains on the other side of the steel panel, the welding quality is good.

    • Take two steel sample plates (consistent with or similar to the materials to be welded).

    • Grind and clean the steel sample plates.

    • Try to weld a welding spot.

    • Check the appearance quality of welding spots.

    • Separate the test welding spots.

    • Measure the diameter of test welding spots.

  • Destructive test methods: Use a sheet metal hammer to knock the chisel between the welding spots, and check the connection of the welding spots. If there is no cracking in the welding spots, restore the panel to its original state with a hammer and bucking block.

    Reminder

    This kind of test is usually carried out on the vehicle body. If the quality is good, restore the panel to its original state.

  • Unqualified welding spots:

    Undesirable phenomena:

    • Spot welding perforation (hole formed by splashing of spot welding under poor welding conditions)

    • Spot welding burrs (serious welding slag burrs formed at the welding spot).

    • Spot welding cracks (cracks in the welding spot caused by poor welding conditions)

    • Spot welding dent (deep welding spot dent caused by poor welding conditions)

    Undesirable case:

    Consequences:

    • The welding strength decreases.

    • The appearance is rough and uneven.

    Solution:

    • Adjust parameters such as welding current and power-on time.

    • Clean the impurities on the welding surface of panels.

    • Trim the flatness of panels to ensure the matching of contact surfaces.

    • Adjust the clamping force of the welder.

  • Qualified welding spots:

    • The welding spot surface shall be smooth and free of obvious splash.

    • The diameter of the weld nugget is moderate.

    • The edge size affected by welding heat is about 20% of the welding spot.

    • There is no obvious burning mark outside the weld nugget.
