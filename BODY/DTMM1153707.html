Masking methods and skills

Caution

  • Cover irrelevant areas of the vehicle according to the requirements of painting.

  • During masking, different materials shall be selected according to different operation requirements.

  • The masking operation shall be standardized and rigorous to avoid damage and looseness after masking.

  • Boundary masking shall be emphasized in work.

  • During operation, please take safety protection measures as required. For details of safety protection, please refer to Spraying protection 

  • Before operation, please carefully read and observe site safety and vehicle protection. For details, please refer to Site safety and vehicle protection 

Masking is required before painting to prevent the mist paint sprayed outside the spraying area from adhering to glass, tire and other non-coated areas. This document introduces the main masking methods used before applying intermediate paint and top coat.

Masking method

Masking of openings

Before spraying, mask the openings left after removal of vehicle body components from the inside or outside to prevent paint from entering the vehicle interior from the openings.

Caution

  • After masking, ensure that the masking tape is not peeled off by air pressure.

  • After masking, ensure that paint mist sprayed does not enter the opening area.

  • The masking tape can be applied either from the inside or from the outside.

  • Mask the inside of the vehicle body opening.

    • Paste the masking material to the inside of the opening.

    • Ensure that the masking tape can withstand external air pressure.

  • Mask the outside of the vehicle body opening: Paste the masking material to the outside of the opening. The masking materials must be pasted tightly and firmly.

Masking of gaps

Before spraying, effectively mask the gaps between vehicle body steel panels. In the masking process, select appropriate masking materials according to the situation and ensure the masking quality.

  • Mask the gap with adhesive tape.

    • Clean the gap first to ensure firm bonding.

    • Ensure that paint mist does not enter the gap after adhesion.

    • When using gap tape, ensure that there is no gap between the tape and the steel panel, and pay special attention to the position of the upper and lower edges when masking the gap at the front and rear doors.

  • Mask the gap with masking paper.

    • Clean the gap first to ensure firm bonding.

    • Ensure that the moisture inside the gap has been removed before masking the trim strip.

    • Cut appropriate length of masking paper and paste it along the front door sealant and the rear door edge steps to ensure good edge masking. Pay attention to the treatment of gap edge during the masking process.

Masking of assembly parts

  • Mask the assembly parts that cannot be disassembled to prevent them from paint adhesion.

  • Reserve a gap with the same coating thickness between the steel panel surface and the masking tape.

    Caution

    • The gap between the masking tape and the steel panel surface is too large, and the masked parts will adhere to paint after spraying.

    • The reserved gap is too small, which will lead to incomplete paint masking or no paint adhesion after edge spraying.

  • Never pull the masking tape when pasting it to the bent part.

  • Finally, mask the boundary of the assembly parts with masking tape.

Masking of glass protective strip

  • To ensure that there is a gap between the glass sealing strip and the steel panel, insert the window edge masking tape between the two and pull it back, so that the sealing strip will be pulled away from the steel panel.

  • For curved areas, cut a few notches on the tape with scissors before pasting the window edge masking tape. Bend the window edge tape along the edge, and use tape to supplement the missing parts.

  • After spraying and drying, when there is still residual heat, remove the window edge tape, and the edge sealing strip will recover to its original shape.

Masking skills

  • Reverse masking method: It is to reversely fold the masking paper when pasting. The purpose is to minimize the steps generated by spraying.

    • If there is vehicle body ridge line in the masking area, reverse masking can be performed on the vehicle body ridge line.

    • It is difficult to clean and degrease after reverse masking, so it is necessary to prepare for degreasing and cleaning before masking.

    • Spraying of intermediate paint The reverse masking method is often used before .

  • Masking of transition area: It refers to masking the area where transition operation is to be carried out, such as rear fender or small-area local repair spraying.

    • After spraying, ensure that the masked boundary surface does not produce steps or color difference.

    • It is difficult to degrease and clean the transition area after masking, so it is necessary to degrease and clean well before masking.
