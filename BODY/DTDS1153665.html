About this manual

  1. This manual, targeted for sheet metal painting technicians, mainly provides guidance on ATTO 3 sheet metal painting.

  2. This manual is provided by the manufacturer and is intended for qualified professional technicians. Attempting to perform any repair without appropriate training may pose a risk of injury to oneself or others.

  3. This manual contains various tips such as "DANGER", "WARNING" and "CAUTION". Please observe them during operation to avoid the risk of hurting yourself, others or vehicle equipment. For details, please refer to Safety description 

  4. For easy understanding, all illustrations in this manual only show the body-in-white (without interior and exterior trims, powertrain, chassis and other systems). If any removal or installation beyond the body-in-white is involved in daily repair, electromechanical technician(s) may need to operate by referring to "ATTO 3 Service Manual".

  5. The directory structure of this manual is mainly divided into 8 parts: About this manual, How to use this manual, Safety and protection, Understanding operation tools, How to perform sheet metal painting for vehicle body, Vehicle identification information, Vehicle body data and structural information, and Replacement of vehicle body sheet metal parts. The contents expressed are as follows:

    • About this manual: It covers the purpose, applicable objects, safety tips of this manual, as well as architecture descriptions that are convenient for sheet metal painting technicians to understand.

    • How to use this manual: It explains the meanings of illustrations, DANGER, WARNING, CAUTION, warm tips and technical terms in this manual.

    • Safety and protection: It includes important safety information about emergency treatment, personnel protection and site protection during operation.

    • Tools and auxiliary materials: It introduces the types and usage of sheet metal tools, spraying tools, sheet metal auxiliary materials and spraying auxiliary materials.

    • How to perform sheet metal painting for vehicle body: Repair technicians can learn the operation process and method of sheet metal and spraying through this content.

      Reminder

      The example drawings in this section are for reference only, and the actual operation position shall prevail.

    • Vehicle identification information: It includes the description and positions of VIN and vehicle business card.

    • Vehicle body data and structural information: It includes important information such as vehicle body dimensions, vehicle body flushness and vehicle body structure required for sheet metal operation.

    • Replacement of vehicle body sheet metal parts: It includes the guidance method for replacing sheet metal parts in common operation scenarios to assist technicians in sheet metal operation.

  6. This manual is mainly used to assist sheet metal painting technicians in repair operation, so as to improve operation efficiency and quality.
