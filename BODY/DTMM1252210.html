Position and installation method of logo

Reminder

This document shows the position of trunk lid logo of this model in different areas. Before installation, look for relevant information according to the style in your area.

Installation skills of logo

Caution

  • Before installation, please confirm whether the paint surface is completely dry. Refer to Step I: Evaluation of spray-painted surface in Performing polishing 

  • Be careful when measuring to avoid scratching the paint surface.

  • Before installing the logo, please ensure that the mounting surface is clean and free of dust, water stains, oil stains, etc.

Reminder

Here, we take the ATTO 3 logo as an example. The installation method of other logos is similar to this.

Tools required:

  • Measuring ruler: No error is required.

  • Masking tape: It should be torn easily, without glue left.

Installation skills

  1. After measuring the logo position with a measuring ruler, mark the corresponding position with the masking tape with a width of about 10 mm.

  2. Align the logo with the marked position and install it.

  3. Carefully remove the masking tape used for marking.
