Grinding

Reminder

This document introduces the auxiliary materials involved in grinding. The specific brands and models of auxiliary materials shall be subject to the actual condition.

Round sandpaper

  • Purpose: It is used to remove the vehicle paint film or grind poly-putty in conjunction with Grinding tools .

  • Instructions for use: For deep scratches that do not cause metal deformation and cracking of the paint coating, use a double-action grinder in conjunction with P240-320 round sandpaper to remove the paint film.

Square sandpaper

  • Purpose: It is used to grind poly-putty in conjunction with an orbital grinder  for shaping.

Scouring pad

  • Purpose: It is used to grind small spaces such as corners and gaps.
