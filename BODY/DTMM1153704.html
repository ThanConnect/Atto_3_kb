Colored paint

Plain paint

  • Characteristic: The paint coating shall be consistent in color when viewed from the front and side, without any particle flickering when observed in sunlight.

  • Ingredient: mainly tinting pigments and resins.

  • Process:

    • After a single-process plain paint is sprayed, the paint coating provides covering power and brightness without the need to spray a separate layer of varnish.

    • For the two-process plain paint, the varnish coating shall be sprayed after the base coat spraying to provide the film brightness.

    • The paint coating process for the plain paint is usually: 3C3B or 4C3B.

Silver paint

  • Characteristic:

    • It gives off glittering metallic luster in sunlight, and the colors of the front and side surfaces are quite different.

    • The aluminum pigments in the aluminum powder paint are completely light reflecting.

  • Ingredient: The aluminum powder paint includes tinting pigments and aluminum pigments.

  • Process: The original paint coating process for the aluminum powder paint is usually: 4C3B.

Pearl paint

  • Characteristic:

    • It presents a deep transparent effect and soft glitter in the sunlight and a plain paint effect indoors.

    • In direct sunlight, it gives a blurry soft shimmer.

    • It has excellent transparency and deepness.

  • Ingredient: The coating includes tinting pigments and pearl mica pigments.

  • Process: The original paint coating process for the pearl paint is usually: 4C3B or 5C3B.

Pearl aluminum paint

  • Characteristic:

    • It presents a soft and blurry metal glitter effect in the sunlight, and also has a metal glitter effect indoors.

    • In well-lit areas, it gives a blurry softer metallic shimmer than that just containing aluminum pigments.

  • Ingredient: It includes tinting pigments, titanium film mica pigments and aluminum pigments.

  • Process: The original paint coating process is usually: 4C3B.
