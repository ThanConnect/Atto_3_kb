Understanding energy-absorbing device

To ensure the safety of occupants as much as possible, a vehicle features the corresponding energy-absorbing device to buffer the impact force as much as possible.

  • Characteristic:

    • To better protect the personal safety of passengers, the impact force is guided through the stress variation design.

    • After being stressed, the energy-absorbing structure absorbs huge impact force through easy deformation.

    • After being buffered by the front and rear energy-absorbing structures of the vehicle body, the inertial impact force and the possibility of passenger compartment deformation are reduced to effectively protect the safety of passengers.

    • This design will enable a transfer of the impact force as designed.

    • This design is available in the front and rear of the body.

  • Design: general use of origami and hole structure design
