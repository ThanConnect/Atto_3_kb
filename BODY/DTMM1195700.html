Cleaning auxiliary materials

Reminder

This document introduces the auxiliary material involved in cleaning. The specific brands and models of auxiliary materials shall be subject to the actual situation.

Tack cloth

  • Purpose: It is used to remove foreign matters or dust from the sprayed surface.

  • Instructions for use: To use a piece of tack cloth, spread it flat, and then fold it again to make it soft.

Degreaser

  • Purpose: It is used to clean and degrease the panel.

  • Instructions for use:

    • Spray the degreaser with a solvent spray pot, then wipe the panel first with a piece of wet wiping cloth, and then dry the panel with a piece of dry wiping cloth.

    • For use, select the appropriate degreaser according to the spraying environment in the spray booth and the paint type.

Anti-static detergent

  • Purpose: It is used to remove static electricity on the panel surface and prevent absorbed dust under static electricity from producing dust spots during painting.

  • Instructions for use: For use, clean the panel surface by alternate wet-dry wiping.

Fine fiber cloth

  • Purpose: It is used to clean the panel surface and narrow areas unaccessible to the polishing machine.
