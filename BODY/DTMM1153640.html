Spraying of intermediate paint

Caution

  • During operation, please take safety protection measures as required. For details of safety protection, please refer to Spraying protection 

  • Before operation, please carefully read and observe site safety and vehicle protection. For details, please refer to Site safety and vehicle protection 

The application of intermediate paint primer shall be carried out before the top coat is applied to:

  • To protect the metal substrate, prevent the substrate metal from being exposed, and improve its rust resistance.

  • To improve adhesion by grinding the surface of the steel panel.

  • To restore surface shape by repairing slight deformation.

  • To prevent poly-putty from absorbing the top coat and affecting the brightness of paint surface.

Operation method

  1. Apply the poly-putty. Refer to Application of sheet metal poly-putty 

  2. Clean and degrease.

    Caution

    Inadequate cleaning can result in peeling or blistering of the coating.

    • Clean the panel with two degreasing cloths: wet cloth and dry cloth. Or operate with a solvent spray pot.

    • After removing all dirt or dust on the surface of the panel with the wiping paper and detergent, blow air with a high-pressure pneumatic gun to the panel surface to remove dust.

  3. Cover the area that does not need to be sprayed. Refer to Masking methods and skills 

  4. Mix intermediate paint. Operation instructions:

    • According to the mixing ratio of the paint manufacturer, select the thinner suitable for the spraying environment, and stir it evenly with the primer and curing agent.

    • Filter the evenly stirred primer through a paper funnel and then pour it into the spray gun.

  5. Apply intermediate paint: Apply the intermediate paint in several coatings, and ensure that the primer is sprayed in the ground area and the poly-putty is completely covered. Operation instructions:

    • Adjust the spray gun to set the spray pattern, paint output, and spraying pressure, and test whether the spray pattern is normal.

    • Apply the first layer of intermediate paint to the area ground with sandpaper #320 until a wet coating is formed in the area.

    • Reduce the area applied with intermediate paint layer by layer during each application. Reserve sufficient flash-off time during application.

    • Confirm the effect after the intermediate paint is applied. In case of pinholes or sandpaper marks, perform local spraying.

  6. Dry the intermediate paint: Forcibly dry the intermediate paint, and start grinding after ensuring that the intermediate paint is completely dry. Operation instructions:

    • 10 to 20 mins reserved before forced drying to allow the solvents in the intermediate paint to volatilize naturally.

    • Before drying the intermediate paint, a curing time shall be reserved. Forced drying without curing time will lead to pinholes.

    • The distance between the infrared heating lamp and the panel shall not be less than 80cm to avoid excessive temperature of the panel.

  7. Check the surface and apply nitrocellulose poly-putty as appropriate. Operating instructions:

    • Check the intermediate paint for defects in a well-lit environment.

    • If there are no pinholes or sandpaper marks, then it is unnecessary to repair the poly-putty.

    • If there are defects such as sand holes or sandpaper marks in the intermediate paint applied area, apply nitrocellulose poly-putty for filling. Operation instructions:

      • Apply the nitrocellulose poly-putty into the pinholes and sandpaper marks, ensuring that all pinholes and sandpaper marks are filled.

      • Press firmly and scrape along the surface with a vertical scraper to apply a thin layer of poly-putty.

  8. Dry the nitrocellulose poly-putty according to the drying conditions of the paint manufacturer.

  9. Manually grind the intermediate paint and poly-putty with abrasive block equipped with sandpaper #320. Operation instructions:

    Caution

    If wet grinding is adopted for the intermediate paint, sandpaper #800 shall be used. The related operation steps and key points are similar.

    • Protect the area around the repaired part with masking tape.

    • In the initial stage of grinding, use slightly coarse sandpaper for initial grinding, and then use fine sandpaper for grinding to ensure a smooth surface and no steps around the edges and surroundings of the intermediate paint.

    • Grind from the intermediate paint to the old coating.

    • Use a vacuum type hand planer to grind the intermediate paint surface. When the sandpaper is blocked, use a brush to remove the grinding dust.

    • To prevent leaving coarse sandpaper marks, ensure that the grinding block is at an angle of 45° to the grinding direction.

    • Do not expand the grinding area when spraying locally.

    • Ensure that all pinholes and sandpaper marks in primer coating are filled.

    • Ensure that the poly-putty is not exposed after grinding the intermediate paint area.

  10. Grind the intermediate paint with a grinding head #3 equipped with sandpaper #400-500 and a double-action grinder. Operation instructions:

    Caution

    • When grinding the exposed poly-putty parts, apply the intermediate paint again, which will absorb the paint and lead to defects.

    • Smooth surface indicates that the grinding of the intermediate paint is completed. Otherwise, the intermediate paint needs to be ground by hand.

    • Check whether the surface is flat with a guide coat during grinding.

    • Before grinding, adjust and test the speed of the grinder. The grinder with a higher speed will be difficult to operate and easy to wear through the intermediate paint coating.

    • Grind from the intermediate paint to the old coating.

    • Manually grind areas where the double-action grinder cannot be used.

    • Do not expand the grinding area during local repair spraying.

    • Ensure a smooth surface and no steps around the edges and surroundings of the intermediate paint, fill all pinholes and sandpaper marks and ensure the poly-putty is not exposed.
