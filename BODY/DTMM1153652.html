FAQs and their solutions

Reminder

This document summarizes the common problems and solutions that may be encountered during masking.

Paint adhesion

  • Cause:

    • The area covered by the masking material before spraying is too small.

    • There are gaps at the edges of panels or gap areas during masking.

  • Solution:

    • Polish the surface after removing paint adhesion. Refer to Performing polishing 

    • Wipe with solvent before the paint is completely dry.

Coating steps

  • Cause:

    • During the application of intermediate paint, reverse masking is not performed in the area, resulting in coating steps after spraying.

    • The spraying range is not reasonably controlled during spraying operation, resulting in an excessively large spraying area.

  • Solution:

    • Reasonably plan the application range before the primer spraying.

    • After thorough grinding, repaint the intermediate paint application area. Refer to Primer spraying 

Masking material adhesion

  • Cause:

    • During the application of intermediate paint, reverse masking is not performed in the area, resulting in coating steps after spraying.

    • The spraying range is not reasonably controlled during spraying operation, resulting in an excessively large spraying area.

  • Solution:

    • Reasonably plan the application range before the primer spraying.

    • After thorough grinding, repaint the intermediate paint application area. Refer to Primer spraying 

Dirt or dust adhesion

  • Cause:

    • The paint and dirt that gather at the folding of the masking material adhere to the surface.

    • The dirt and dust remaining in the gaps between the panels adhere to the surface.

    • There are steps during masking.

    • Gaps between panels are not completely masked.

  • Solution: Grind the area with sandpaper to eliminate defects, and then polish the area. Refer to Performing polishing 

Tape marks

  • Cause:

    • The residue of adhesive tape affects the paint film.

    • The surface temperature of the panel is too high or the masking tape comes into contact with solvent, resulting in adhesive falling off.

    • No suitable sticking material is selected.

  • Solution:

    • Remove the tape before the surface dries.

    • Polish the surface for repair. Refer to Performing polishing 

Boundary paint falling off

  • Cause:

    • After forced drying, the masking tape is removed after the surface cools down. Improper operation method is used for cleaning.

    • The surface of the substrate is not properly ground and cleaned before spraying.

    • The paint coating is too thick, and the method of masking tape removal is incorrect.

    • The angle is incorrect when the tape is removed.

  • Solution: Grind the defect and repaint it. Refer to:

    • Solvent-based plain paint 

    • Solvent-based metallic paint 

    • Spraying of plastic parts
