Function of polishing

  • Possible defects on the coating surface can be repaired by polishing in daily operation, such as sagging, dust spots, orange peel, gloss loss, poor film fullness, and poor transition at the junction of new and old films.

  • It can increase the glossiness and smoothness of paint films, and eliminate fine defects on paint surfaces such as particles, slight flow marks, orange peel, fine sandpaper marks, and scratches.

  • It can be used for re-painting of old paintwork.

Polishing principle

  • Causes of roughness and unevenness of the coating surface: The gloss of a paint coating is generated by the reflection of light, so the light diffuses and reflects on the rough surface, resulting in numerous visible small scratches and a lack of gloss.

  • Polishing principle: reduce diffuse reflection on the surface and make the coating surface have uniform brightness.

Essentials of polishing

  • Polish with proper force, abrasive (polishing wax) and speed.

  • The "frictional heat" and "static electricity" generated by friction of the polishing pad on the paint film are also factors that adversely affect the polishing.

  • Quality requirement: Make the texture and glossiness of the repainted surface consistent with those of the original surface.

  • Operation environment: The workshop operation environment shall be well lighted for convenience of paint film inspection. Besides, a clean operation environment is required to avoid surface damage caused by dust.

  • Contact position: During operation, the polishing machine shall be slightly inclined to make the polishing pad lightly contact the surface. Do not let the whole polishing pad contact the surface.

    Caution

    To avoid deformation of the steel panel or condensation of polishing wax, spray water on the steel panel during operation.
